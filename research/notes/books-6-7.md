Important constraints for reconciliation and modelling:

- **Secret passages:** Neville says all seven old passages were sealed before
  the 1997–98 school year, with cursed entrances and watched exits
  (B07-C029-P0011, B07-C029-P0012). The trio subsequently encounter no barrier
  in the Willow passage (B07-C032-P0114), apart from a crate at its Shack end
  (B07-C032-P0118). Preserve the blanket statement as a report and the later
  journey as an observation. Do not invent an unnoticed unsealing event.
- **Changing refuge routes:** The refuge's cupboard staircase changes its
  castle endpoint daily; its observed exit is on the fifth floor that night
  (B07-C029-P0143, B07-C029-P0144, B07-C029-P0146 through P0149). That does not
  relocate every Room of Requirement configuration to the fifth floor. The
  familiar three-pass wall request is subsequently used for the hiding room
  (B07-C031-P0196 through P0198). The refuge's bathroom and Hog's Head supply
  tunnel are configuration-specific.
- **After Fiendfyre:** The hiding-room fire consumes much accumulated material,
  and its door vanishes after the escape. Ron asks whether the Room can still
  work (B07-C031-P0258, B07-C031-P0259, B07-C031-P0263, B07-C031-P0264). No answer
  establishes permanent loss of every possible configuration.
- **Hagrid's hut:** Book 6 leaves the wooden cabin a smoking wreck after its
  fire (B06-C028-P0061 through P0067). Book 7 reports a gathering in Hagrid's
  house (B07-C022-P0163) and later directly shows his dark hut standing on the
  route to the Forest (B07-C034-P0047, B07-C034-P0048). Repair or replacement is
  not narrated. Earlier furniture cannot automatically be assumed to survive.
- **Tomb and wand:** The tomb forms from white marble at the funeral, then is
  split lengthwise in book 7 (B06-C030-P0061, B06-C030-P0062;
  B07-C024-P0252 through P0255). Harry announces that he will return the Elder
  Wand to its source (B07-C036-P0192). Neither a completed return nor tomb
  repair is shown. The real sword's separate hidden store is explicitly a
  cavity behind Dumbledore's movable office portrait (B07-C033-P0352,
  B07-C033-P0353); this does not date the display-case substitution.
- **Apparition:** Wizard prohibition covers Hogwarts grounds; house-elves are
  an explicit exception (B07-C010-P0157). Outdoor Disapparition fails in
  Hogsmeade (B07-C028-P0016, B07-C028-P0017), while direct arrival into the
  Hog's Head bar and evacuation from it are described as viable
  (B07-C029-P0006; B07-C030-P0064 through P0068). Do not flatten these distinct
  destinations and wartime states into a single universal town rule.
- **Levels and bearings:** Keep unnumbered corridors, unnamed classrooms and
  ambiguous stair sequences unnumbered. In particular, the sixth-floor boys'
  bathroom approach uses a staircase called marble without identifying it as
  the Entrance Hall's principal stair (B06-C024-P0060, B06-C024-P0061,
  B06-C024-P0106). The
  Ravenclaw dormitories initially guessed above the statue-side door are
  confirmed by sleepers descending from above in the next chapter
  (B07-C029-P0164; B07-C030-P0004, B07-C030-P0014). North is explicit for the
  threatened battlements (B07-C031-P0190), not for every nearby window.
- **Burial recollection for parent cross-check:** Harry recalls Crouch's bone
  burial in Hagrid's front garden (B07-C006-P0080). Compare this wording with
  the original book 4 passage in the parent's catalogue; no remembered
  correction or exact grave position has been imported here.
- **Remote water bodies:** The sea-cave lake, Forest of Dean pool, Gringotts
  underground lake and dragon's unnamed mountain landing lake are separate
  from the school lake. Their complete passages were read, but their terrain
  was excluded. Similarly the bright King's Cross-like space in chapter 35
