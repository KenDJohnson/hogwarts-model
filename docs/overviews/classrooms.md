# Classrooms and teaching spaces

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/classrooms.md)

Teaching rooms run through the whole height of the castle. Below ground is the Potions dungeon, one storey under the Entrance Hall. On the ground floor, classroom eleven and other little-used rooms open off the corridor opposite the Great Hall. The first floor has several ordinary classrooms near the head of the marble staircase. The third floor holds the Defence classroom, four floors below the Gryffindor common room, probably the Charms classroom, and an empty room beside the one-eyed witch. History of Magic and Transfiguration lie on unstated upper floors. The disused Mirror of Erised room is around the fourth floor, and more classrooms lie on the east wing's fifth floor. North Tower's round Divination room tops the sequence, above the seventh floor. A normal class is two houses of one year, about twenty pupils, and only the Divination room has a table count. Rooms are numbered to at least eleven, and many stand empty.

## Size and numbers

- **Inference.** A normal joint-house class is about twenty pupils. Because: Twenty brooms are laid out for Gryffindor and Slytherin first-years, twenty cauldrons for the same pair in 1992, and about twenty pairs of earmuffs for a Herbology class. (B01-041, B02-020, B02-073)
- **Explicit.** Counts the text gives: twenty cauldrons in Potions, at least twenty tables in Divination, twelve pupils at three tables in N.E.W.T. Potions. No other room has a seat count. (B02-073, B03-017, B06-032)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 50 sq m for an ordinary classroom. Because: Twenty desks at an assumed 1.5 sq m each is 30 sq m; aisles, the teacher's end and a blackboard add about 20 sq m. (B01-041, B02-073)
- **Estimate.** Allow for at least a dozen teaching rooms in use at the same time. Because: Seven year groups, each split into two joint-house classes, gives up to fourteen classes at once. Assumes greenhouses and lawns take only a few of them. (B01-041, B05-191)
- **Inference.** Rooms are numbered in series: classroom eleven and dungeon five imply at least eleven classrooms and five dungeons. Because: A numbered name implies lower numbers exist, though none of the others is ever listed. (B05-191, B02-036)

## Where it is

- **Inference.** Floors stated or implied: Potions below ground; classroom eleven ground; Defence and probably Charms third; Mirror room about fourth; classrooms on the fifth, east wing; Divination above seventh. Because: Each is worked through in its own overview. Transfiguration and History of Magic have no stated floor. (B05-110, B05-191, B06-027, B01-050, B01-081, B05-235, B03-111)

## Open questions

- Transfiguration and History of Magic have no stated floor and need a modelling decision.
- The Defence and Charms classrooms may share the third floor, close to the Defence office, but the text never relates the two classrooms.

## Potions classroom

The Potions classroom is one of the large dungeons under the castle, one storey below the ground floor, entered off a dim stone passage at the foot of steps from the Entrance Hall. It is colder than the castle above, with a stone floor and pickled animals in glass jars all round the walls. In a normal lesson twenty cauldrons stand among wooden desks or shared tables, with stools for the pupils and a small fire under each cauldron. The teacher's desk and a blackboard stand at the front, a stone basin fed by a gargoyle spout sits in one corner, and an ingredient store cupboard opens from the room. A memory from fifty years before Book 2 shows the same dungeon. It stays the Potions room under Slughorn from 1996, when Snape moves upstairs to teach Defence.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** A normal 1992 lesson has twenty steaming cauldrons standing among the wooden desks. (B02-073)
- **Explicit.** The 1996 N.E.W.T. class is twelve pupils at three tables of four. (B06-032)
- **Inference.** The room must seat a joint class of about twenty, each with a cauldron, with walking room between lines of desks. Because: Twenty cauldrons in 1992 and twenty brooms for the same two houses in 1991; Umbridge walks between two lines of desks to reach a pupil's cauldron. (B01-041, B02-073, B05-094)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 70 sq m of floor. Because: Twenty pupils at tables of three or four is about six tables. At an assumed 6 sq m per table with stools and cauldrons that is 36 sq m; aisles, teacher's desk and side fittings roughly double it. (B02-073)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It is one of the large dungeons, down stone steps from the Entrance Hall, and colder than the castle above. (B01-034, B01-036, B02-073, B03-027, B05-072)
- **Inference.** It lies one storey below the ground floor. Because: The dungeons are eight floors below the seventh floor (B05-C018-P0190), and a single run of stone steps links them to the Entrance Hall. (B05-072, B05-093, B05-110)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The floor is stone, and the room is dim enough to have a dark corner where an inspector sits unnoticed at first. (B01-034, B05-094)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A dim stone passage runs outside, where voices echo and classes queue at a door that creaks open and bangs shut. (B04-069, B05-034, B05-035, B05-093, B05-094)
- **Explicit.** A concealed short cut, entered by a door posing as solid wall, runs towards the room. (B06-113)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Pickled animals in glass jars stand all round the walls. (B01-034)
- **Explicit.** A teacher's desk and blackboard stand at the front; pupils use wooden desks or shared tables and stools, with some tables at the back. (B02-073, B04-069, B04-173, B05-035, B06-100, B02-051, B05-228)
- **Explicit.** Brass scales and ingredient jars stand on the desks, and each cauldron sits over its own small adjustable fire. (B02-073, B05-036, B06-100)
- **Explicit.** A stone basin in one corner takes an icy jet from a gargoyle's mouth, for washing hands and ladles. (B03-026)
- **Explicit.** An ingredient store cupboard holds unicorn horns, dried herbs and a box of bezoars; a corner cupboard keeps spare textbooks and old scales. (B05-035, B06-032, B06-100, B02-063)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Snape teaches here until 1996, then Slughorn; Snape keeps his dungeon office. (B06-031, B06-160)
- **Reported.** A memory from fifty years before Book 2 shows the same dungeon, torches unlit, with a door that can stand almost closed. (B02-105)
- **Explicit.** Accidents such as a melted cauldron and spilled potion are cleared by wand and leave no recorded damage. (B01-035, B02-074)
- **Explicit.** September 1996: extra cauldrons of finished potions stand near the tables and on the teacher's desk. (B06-033)

### Open questions

- Layout: the text uses desks, tables, lines and a usual table at the back without giving one plan. Shared tables for three or four, in lines, fit all of it.
- Whether the corner cupboard and the ingredient store cupboard are one fixture or two.

## Defence Against the Dark Arts classroom

The Defence Against the Dark Arts classroom is a conventional teaching room used by every Defence teacher from 1991. No passage names its floor, but it lies four floors below the Gryffindor common room, which makes it the third floor. Desks stand in rows with chairs and some stools, facing a teacher's desk and a blackboard. There is a back row beside a window, more windows with curtains, a ceiling candelabrum and wall space for pictures. One window shows the top of a ship's sail on the lake. The desks can be swept aside to leave a large clear floor for a whole class to practise. The corridor outside holds a queue, and a side passage opens nearby. The room is separate from the teacher's office: nothing in the text joins them, and pupils reach the office by its own corridor.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** No desk count is given. Desks stand in rows, with at least a front and a back row, and must seat a joint class of about twenty. Because: Rows are named in B02-C006-P0135 and B05-C015-P0112. Twenty is the size of a two-house class elsewhere: twenty brooms and twenty cauldrons. (B02-023, B05-074, B01-041, B02-073)
- **Explicit.** Moody clears the desks with one wand stroke to leave a large open floor in the middle, where the class takes turns. (B04-036)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It is on the third floor, four floors below the seventh-floor common room. Because: B06-C009-P0041 gives four floors below the common room; the Fat Lady's entrance is on the seventh floor (B01-C009-P0200); seven minus four is three. (B06-027, B01-046)
- **Inference.** It lies below the fifth floor. Because: During the east-wing diversion that left a swamp on the fifth floor, pupils outside the classroom hear the noise from above and look up at the ceiling. (B05-230, B05-235)
- **Explicit.** A corridor runs past the door and holds a queue; a side passage with a stone wall opens nearby, with a corner beyond. (B04-031, B06-027)
- **Inference.** One window faces the lake. Because: The top sail of the Durmstrang ship, moored on the lake, shows through it (B04-C029-P0090). (B04-194)
- **Explicit.** In 1995 pupils arrive here ten minutes after the bell at the top of North Tower, by way of the ladder and stairs. (B05-073)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** There are several windows that can be curtained, one beside the back row; winter sun reaches into the room. (B02-023, B03-055, B06-028)
- **Explicit.** A candelabrum hangs from the ceiling; in 1992 it gave way under a swinging pupil. (B02-023, B02-024)
- **Inference.** The text treats it as one fixed room across teachers, though less directly than it does the office. Because: Each year it is simply the Defence classroom, and in 1996 it is gloomier than usual, which compares it with its earlier self (B06-C009-P0047). Nothing says the post changed rooms. (B01-032, B02-023, B04-030, B05-041, B06-028)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** In 1992 the way in runs from a courtyard side door along a corridor and up one staircase. (B02-022)
- **Inference.** The classroom and office are separate rooms; the text describes no door or stair joining them. Because: In 1996 Harry leaves the classroom and runs through other corridors to reach the office corridor (B05-C029-P0175 to P0179). Moody offers to take Neville up to his office. Snape teaches here from a dungeon office. (B04-031, B05-230, B06-160)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Furniture: a wooden teacher's desk with a drawer at the front, a blackboard, pupil desks with chairs and some stools, ink bottles and a waste bin. (B02-023, B04-030, B05-041, B05-074, B06-029)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** 1991: the room smells strongly of garlic under Quirrell. (B01-032)
- **Explicit.** 1992: pixies wreck it, breaking a window, tearing down pictures and scattering papers. (B02-024)
- **Explicit.** 1996-97 under Snape: curtains drawn, candles for light, and new wall pictures of people suffering curses and injuries. (B06-028, B06-029)
- **Reported.** Dumbledore says that since Voldemort was refused the post no Defence teacher has lasted more than a year. The curse attaches to the job, not to any fitting in the room. (B06-123)

### Conflicts

- Floor. Four floors below the common room gives the third. But Harry runs down only the nearest flight of stairs to McGonagall's first-floor door, which suggests the second; one staircase leads up from a courtyard side door, which suggests the first; and Lupin's class walks to the staff room with no stairs mentioned. (B06-027, B05-042, B01-103, B02-022, B03-028)
- Moody offers to take Neville up to his office from beside the classroom, so the office should be higher, yet the explicit floors put both on the third. The office's fourth-floor hints would fit this. (B04-031, B06-027, B05-055)

### Open questions

- research/places.json describes the teacher's office as adjoining this classroom. No passage supports that, so do not model a connecting door or stair.
- Use the third floor unless the classroom's position beside the office is settled another way.

## Transfiguration classroom

The Transfiguration classroom is McGonagall's teaching room, on an unstated floor above the ground floor. It has pupil desks, a teacher's desk, a blackboard and windows looking down into the grounds, with enough open floor in the middle for spell practice when the room is empty. In 1992 it lay close enough to a corridor of classrooms for her class to run out to an attack there, and that corridor is one corner from the Headmaster's gargoyle. In 1993 pupils struggled to find it after North Tower, and Ron later called it a very long way from Umbridge's office.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** No desk count is given. It must seat a joint class of about twenty, at desks reaching to seats at the back. Because: Pupils have their own desks and there are back seats. Twenty is the size of a two-house class elsewhere. (B02-131, B04-111, B01-041, B02-073)
- **Explicit.** Emptied at lunchtime in 1995, it gives three pupils open floor in the middle for spell practice. (B04-205, B05-085)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It stands above ground level, with a window looking down on a tree in the grounds, close enough to watch a pupil's hand gestures. Because: Pupils stare down from the window at Malfoy under a tree below and can make out what he does with his hand (B04-C031-P0027 to P0029). (B04-205)
- **Explicit.** A girls' bathroom lies on the floor below. (B06-082)
- **Inference.** It is close to the dark corridor where two victims were found in December 1992. Because: McGonagall and her class come running as soon as the alarm is raised (B02-C011-P0184). (B02-081)
- **Explicit.** In 1992 Harry passes it on his way from Gryffindor Tower to the library. (B02-078)
- **Explicit.** In 1993 pupils leaving North Tower take so long to find it that they only just arrive on time. (B03-019)
- **Reported.** Ron calls the Transfiguration department miles from Umbridge's office. (B05-271)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Teacher's desk, pupil desks and a blackboard readable from the back; a corner has room for an inspector's seat. (B01-031, B02-131, B04-111, B05-075)
- **Explicit.** Lesson props include a large cage of guinea-fowl, boxes of mice and, by 1996, mirrors for human transfiguration. (B04-111, B05-075, B06-082)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Conjured birds in 1996 and an escaped firework dragon in 1996 fly about the room without lasting effect. (B05-217, B06-068)

### Open questions

- Floor is not given. Any floor from the first upward fits, provided a girls' bathroom lies on the floor beneath.

## Charms classroom

The Charms classroom is Flitwick's teaching room on the Charms corridor, reached from the Entrance Hall by the marble staircase and further corridors. No passage states its floor. Book 1 implies the third, because the corridor ends at a door into the forbidden third-floor corridor. Pupils sit at tables, and objects can be sent flying across the room into a box on the far side. The room has a chandelier, windows overlooking the front drive, a large cabinet that stores practice cushions, and a teacher's desk that Flitwick can see over only from a pile of books.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** No table count is given. It must seat a joint class of about twenty, each pupil in 1995 with a stack of cushions. Because: Twenty is the size of a two-house class elsewhere. Pupils work at tables, one kept by three friends at the very back. (B04-151, B01-041, B02-073)
- **Explicit.** Objects cross the whole room: pupils Banish cushions into a box on the far side and Summon things to themselves, and a misfire carries Flitwick past the back table. (B04-068, B04-151)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It is probably on the third floor. Because: The Charms corridor ends at a locked door that opens straight into the forbidden third-floor corridor (B01-C009-P0182 to P0196), and pupils are near the classroom when they enter that corridor. (B01-049, B01-050)
- **Explicit.** From the Entrance Hall the way goes up the marble staircase and along corridors. (B01-042, B03-100, B04-084)
- **Explicit.** A tapestry-hidden passage comes out near it, and pupils count it as a very long way from the trophy room. (B01-049)
- **Explicit.** A window overlooks the front drive and the way into the castle. (B05-240)
- **Explicit.** An empty classroom lies further along the same corridor. (B01-042, B01-043)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A chandelier hangs from the ceiling, windows take the rain, and a jet of water or conjured snow can reach the ceiling. (B04-151, B05-100, B06-094, B06-150)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Tables for pupils, and a teacher's desk that Flitwick sees over only by standing on a pile of books. (B01-031, B04-151, B05-050)
- **Explicit.** A large cabinet or cupboard stores the practice cushions; board dusters, wastepaper baskets and Lunascopes are about the room. (B04-068, B04-151, B04-195)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** December 1993: live fairies are hung as Christmas lights. (B03-058)
- **Explicit.** Lessons bring temporary frogs, ravens, walking teacups and false snow; first-year practical exams in 1992 use the teacher's desk. (B01-116, B05-100, B05-241, B06-150)

### Conflicts

- Book 1 joins the Charms corridor to the third-floor forbidden corridor, yet the same night treats Charms as very far from the third-floor trophy room, and in Book 4 the way there starts at the top of the marble staircase, which is the first floor. (B01-049, B01-050, B04-084)

### Open questions

- An unnamed first-floor classroom with a chandelier is used at break straight after a Charms lesson. The text does not say it is Flitwick's room, so keep them apart unless the third-floor reading is dropped.

Lupin judged it big enough for Patronus practice. The corridor outside has a view over the grounds towards Hagrid's cabin.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** No desk count is given. Seats stand in rows, with a way along a row to the window, and must take a joint class of about twenty. Because: Twenty is the size of a two-house class elsewhere. (B05-089, B01-041, B02-073)

### Where it is

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Lamps can be lit by wand; left empty, the room is dark and its floor dusty. (B03-077)

### What is inside

### How it changes

## North Tower and Divination classroom

The Divination classroom is a round room at the very top of North Tower, which is also Sybill Trelawney's home. The route climbs to the seventh floor, passes Sir Cadogan's landing, follows a corridor, goes up a tight spiral stair to a tiny doorless landing, then up a silver ladder through a circular trapdoor in the landing's ceiling. Inside, no fewer than twenty small round tables are crammed among chintz armchairs and pouffes. Closed curtains, scarf-draped lamps and a perfumed fire keep the room dim, red and stifling, and shelves run all round the curved wall. Ron allows ten minutes for the journey from the Great Hall. From 1996 Firenze teaches on the ground floor because a centaur cannot use the ladder, while Trelawney stays in the castle.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The landing is tiny, yet most of a class crowds onto it, and in exam week the queue runs back down the stair. (B03-016, B03-111)
- **Reported.** Ron allows ten minutes for the climb from the Great Hall, and the text calls the journey long. (B03-014)
- **Estimate.** The route from the Great Hall is several hundred metres long. Because: Ten minutes is 600 s. About 8 flights at an assumed 20 s each take under 3 minutes; the remaining 7 minutes at an assumed 1 m/s is about 400 m. (B03-014)
- **Explicit.** At least twenty small round tables are crammed into the room. (B03-017)
- **Inference.** The room can seat far more than one class. Because: Three friends share one table (B03-C006-P0062). Twenty tables at three each is sixty places, against a joint class of about twenty. (B03-017)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 9 m across inside. Because: Twenty tables with their chairs at an assumed 3 sq m each, crammed, is 60 sq m, a circle about 9 m wide, before adding hearth and shelves. (B03-017)
- **Inference.** The room stands above the seventh floor by a whole spiral stair plus a ladder. Because: The stair is long enough to make climbers dizzy (B03-C006-P0049), and the ladder then rises through the landing's ceiling. (B03-016, B03-111)
- **Reported.** Hermione doubts a voice from the top of the tower could carry down to the grounds. (B04-247)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The classroom is at the very top of North Tower. (B03-014, B04-026, B05-039)
- **Explicit.** On the seventh floor pupils are already sitting on the spiral stair that leads up to the room. (B03-111)
- **Inference.** Seven long staircases from the ground floor reach Sir Cadogan's landing, which is on the seventh floor. Because: B03-C006-P0034 counts the staircases; B03-C014-P0002 names the floor of that landing. (B03-014, B03-089)
- **Reported.** Ron says the passage running right from that landing heads south, since a window there shows part of the lake. (B03-014)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The room is circular, with shelves running round the walls, and looks more like a cluttered attic crossed with a tearoom than a classroom. (B03-017, B03-018)
- **Explicit.** Windows are normally curtained, but at least one can be opened an inch for air. (B03-017, B04-027, B04-197)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** From the landing a corridor leads to a narrow, tightly winding spiral stair hung with pictures of crinolined women and sinister monks. (B03-015, B03-016, B04-196)
- **Explicit.** The stair ends at a tiny doorless landing with a sunny window. A circular trapdoor in its ceiling has a brass nameplate and opens from above to lower a silver ladder. (B03-016, B04-026)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Chintz armchairs and fat pouffes crowd the rickety tables, and the furniture blocks any direct path across the room. (B03-017, B03-102, B05-040)
- **Explicit.** Many lamps draped in red scarves give a dim crimson light. (B03-017, B04-027, B05-040)
- **Explicit.** A perfumed fire under a crowded mantelpiece heats a large copper kettle and keeps the room stifling; Trelawney's high-backed winged chair stands beside it. (B03-017, B03-018, B04-027, B05-095, B03-112, B04-104, B04-197)
- **Explicit.** Shelves hold feathers, candle stubs, worn playing cards, silvery crystal balls and a great many teacups; a solar-system model under glass is kept beneath her chair. (B03-018, B04-197)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Trelawney lives in this room and seldom leaves it. (B04-026, B05-173)
- **Explicit.** For crystal-gazing lessons a glowing ball stands on every table. (B03-102)
- **Reported.** After her 1996 dismissal she stays in the castle and pupils visit her upstairs; classes move to classroom eleven, since a centaur cannot use the ladder. (B05-189, B05-190, B05-191)

### Open questions

- The number of storeys between the seventh floor and the room is not given. Allow at least one full storey for the spiral stair, plus the ladder.
- The stair is narrow and the room is wide. Decide whether the stair climbs inside a tower of the room's width or in a thinner turret that opens out at the top.

## Classroom eleven

Classroom eleven is a ground-floor room along the corridor that leaves the Entrance Hall on the side opposite the Great Hall. Before 1996 it was one of several rarely used rooms, with the neglected air of a storeroom. From March 1996 Firenze teaches Divination there, in a room Dumbledore has turned into an indoor forest clearing.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** The school numbers its classrooms, and has at least eleven. Because: The name implies a numbered series; rooms one to ten are never listed. (B05-191)
- **Inference.** No seat count is given. A class sits on the ground among the trees while a centaur stands in the open middle, so it cannot be a small room. Because: The students lean on trunks and boulders, and Firenze stands in a treeless clearing at the centre (B05-C027-P0020). (B05-192)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Ground floor, down a corridor that leaves the Entrance Hall on the far side from the Great Hall. (B05-191)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Before 1996 it was seldom used and felt like a cupboard or storeroom. It has windows, a closing door and a ceiling. (B05-191, B05-192, B05-194)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** The move from North Tower is made because a centaur cannot climb the ladder; Dumbledore says the new teacher prefers lodgings on the ground floor. (B05-189, B05-191)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A wastepaper basket survives beside the stump, and a school bell rings right outside the door. (B05-192, B05-194)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** From March 1996: springy moss and earth for a floor, trees growing from it with branches across ceiling and windows, green dappled light, boulders and a stump. (B05-192)
- **Explicit.** Firenze can dim the room to twilight and show stars overhead, and pupils burn herbs on the floor. (B05-193)

### Open questions

- The text does not say whether Firenze lodges in this room, or whether the forest stays after he shares Divination with Trelawney from 1996.

## Unnamed and unused classrooms

This group gathers classrooms the text does not name. They are not one room. They matter because they show ordinary teaching rooms at many levels: a free ground-floor room for a wet-weather class, several first-floor rooms, a third-floor room beside the one-eyed witch, rooms on the east wing's fifth floor, and others on unstated floors. Typical fittings are desks, chairs, a blackboard, a waste bin, a door that shuts and a window. Many stand empty and unlocked, and pupils and staff borrow them freely.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** One corridor in 1992 has classes on both sides, and door after door opens along it. (B02-081)
- **Inference.** Spare rooms are plentiful: pupils and staff find free ones at short notice in most years. Because: Couples, Harry's practice sessions and private talks all find empty rooms without planning; Harry tries doors along the first-floor corridor and soon finds one. (B02-162, B04-089, B05-270, B06-076)
- **Explicit.** A large classroom holds first-year written exams and grows swelteringly hot. (B01-116)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Ground floor: a free classroom takes a Care of Magical Creatures lesson during a 1995 storm. (B05-107)
- **Explicit.** First floor: an overcrowded room with seats, windows and a chandelier is used at break in 1995; another empty room opens off the corridor from the head of the marble staircase. (B05-101, B05-270)
- **Inference.** First floor, probably: the room holding the wrecked Vanishing Cabinet, directly above Filch's office. Because: Nick has the cabinet crashed in a classroom right over the office, and in 1996 the twins speak of a Vanishing Cabinet on the first floor. Assumes one cabinet, unmoved. (B02-039, B05-209)
- **Inference.** Hermione leaves the others on the first floor for her 1994 Muggle Studies exam, so that room is probably on that floor. Because: The text gives only where she turns off (B03-C016-P0053). (B03-111)
- **Explicit.** Third floor: an empty classroom with desks stands to the left of the one-eyed witch statue. (B03-059)
- **Explicit.** East wing, fifth floor: classrooms lie beyond the corridor flooded in 1996, and Filch punts pupils across to them. (B05-235)
- **Explicit.** Other rooms are tied to neighbours: one floor above Lockhart's office, further along from Charms, and the last on Slughorn's corridor. (B01-042, B02-049, B06-087)
- **Inference.** An unlocked classroom with a teacher's desk lies close to the Fat Lady's corridor, so classrooms probably reach the seventh floor. Because: Harry finds it after stepping out of the portrait hole into a deserted corridor; no stairs are mentioned and the floor is not repeated. (B06-076)
- **Explicit.** Windows differ by room: some look on the lake, some on the grounds, and one towards the perimeter wall. (B04-153, B05-308, B07-060)
