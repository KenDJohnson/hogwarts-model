# Room of Requirement and its changing states

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/room-of-requirement.md)

The Room of Requirement is a room on the seventh floor that appears on need, behind a stretch of blank stone wall in a corridor with an enormous tapestry of Barnabas the Barmy and his ballet-dancing trolls. Walk past the wall three times thinking hard of what you need, turning at the window at one end and the man-sized vase at the other, and a door appears. Behind it the Room becomes what you need, at any size, and its door turns back to stone afterwards. Three states matter. From 1995 it was a spacious torch-lit practice room seating 28 on cushions, with bookcases and Dark detectors. In 1997 and 1998 it was a cathedral-sized store of hidden objects, a city of heaps with one wall fifty feet high, until cursed fire swept it. In spring 1998 it was an enormous panelled refuge with balcony and hammocks, with a passage to the Hog's Head and a stair whose exit moved daily. The largest states cannot physically fit on the seventh floor, so treat the interior as a separate volume.

## Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** Size follows need: a broom cupboard, a room for 28, a cathedral-sized store, and an enormous refuge that grew from one hammock. (B05-111, B05-113, B06-156, B07-047, B07-049)
- **Explicit.** The practice room seats 28 on cushions and takes 14 duelling pairs, but is too tight for all to practise Stunning at once. (B05-111, B05-114, B05-146)
- **Explicit.** The store of hidden things is as big as a large cathedral, with thousands and thousands of books and a heap fifty feet high. (B06-156, B06-157, B07-082)
- **Inference.** The refuge took hundreds passing through on the night of the battle, with more than twenty living there before it. Because: Harry hears hundreds marching towards it and Aberforth reports hundreds through his pub at the far end. (B07-048, B07-069)
- **Inference.** The largest states cannot fit behind a seventh-floor wall, so treat the interior as separate from the castle's plan. Because: The same wall opens on a cupboard or on a space over 15 m high, more than four storeys, and no floor above the seventh is named. (B05-113, B06-156, B07-082)

## Where it is

- **Explicit.** The entrance is on the seventh floor, in the blank wall facing the Barnabas tapestry. (B05-108, B06-125)

## Getting there

- **Explicit.** Walking past three times while concentrating makes a door appear. It turns back to stone when the room is left. (B05-109, B05-116, B06-155)

## Conflicts

- The door is on the seventh floor, yet the 1998 refuge stair runs up to a fifth-floor corridor. (B05-108, B07-051)

## Open questions

- Whether the Room survived the 1998 fire. Ron asks and the books do not answer.

## Seventh-floor corridor

This seventh-floor corridor holds the entrance to the Room of Requirement. On one side hangs an enormous moving tapestry of Barnabas the Barmy trying to teach trolls ballet. Facing it is a stretch of blank stone wall where the Room's door appears, with a window just beyond the stretch and a man-sized vase at its other end. Further along are a boys' bathroom, niches holding vases, and corners where lookouts can wait. The corridor can be walked to along the seventh floor from the Gryffindor portrait; the Headmaster's gargoyle, on the same floor, is on the far side of the castle. In May 1998 a blast tore away the castle wall along it.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The tapestry is enormous and its figures move: trolls in tutus club Barnabas, who is trying to teach them ballet, and a moth-eaten troll pauses to watch passers-by. (B05-108, B06-118, B06-164)
- **Explicit.** A window just beyond the stretch and a man-sized vase at its other end mark the length walked to open the door. The distance is not given. (B05-109)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The Room's entrance is on the seventh floor, facing the tapestry of Barnabas the Barmy. (B05-108, B06-125)
- **Explicit.** The corridor runs both ways from the door. To the right lie a niche under an ugly dragon-shaped vase, a boys' bathroom and another corner. (B05-198, B05-199)
- **Explicit.** Coming round a nearby corner, the tapestry is on your right and the blank wall on your left. The corner gives lookouts somewhere to wait. (B06-125, B06-164, B06-165)
- **Explicit.** It is reached from the Gryffindor portrait by walking along the seventh floor. (B05-108, B06-155, B06-164)
- **Reported.** The Headmaster's gargoyle is also on the seventh floor, on the far side of the castle, though Umbridge marched Harry there from here within minutes. (B05-200, B06-035, B06-128)
- **Explicit.** A boys' bathroom lies a floor below, on the sixth floor, reached by a stair the text calls marble. (B06-152)
- **Inference.** The corridor runs along an outer wall of the castle. Because: It has a window, corridor windows near the refuge stair look down on the castle's foot, and in 1998 a blast here opened it to cold air and a drop. That scene names no floor. (B05-109, B07-080, B07-088, B07-089)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Opposite it is a stretch of smooth, blank stone wall, solid to a push or kick when no door is present. (B05-108, B06-126, B06-164)
- **Explicit.** The Room's door appears in that wall when called and vanishes into the stone afterwards, leaving no sign. (B05-116, B06-155, B07-086)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A large blue-and-white vase stands in a niche nearby. Whether it is one of the other two vases is unknown. (B06-166)

### How it changes

- **Reported.** June 1997: Darkness Powder filled the corridor with black that no light spell could pierce, while Death Eaters left the Room. (B06-194)
- **Explicit.** May 1998: an explosion blew away the castle side, leaving stone and wood wreckage. Giant spiders tried to climb in through the hole, and the windows lost their glass. (B07-087, B07-088, B07-089)

### Conflicts

- Portrait and corridor are both on the seventh floor, yet in 1995 the walk between them passes the top of a final staircase. (B01-053, B05-108)
- A 1997 route from the hospital wing down to breakfast passes through a seventh-floor corridor with this tapestry, which puts the ward at or above the seventh floor unless the route doubled back. (B06-118)

### Open questions

- Whether the blank stretch backs on to the outer wall. If the window beyond it is in the same wall, nothing ordinary can stand behind the door.

## Room of Requirement: how it behaves

The Room of Requirement, also called the Come and Go Room, is a room on the seventh floor that exists only for someone who needs it. To open it you walk three times past a blank stretch of wall, concentrating on what you need. A polished door with a brass handle then appears, and it melts back into stone when the room is left. Inside, the Room supplies what the seeker needs, at any size: it has been a broom cupboard, a room of chamber pots, a practice room for 28, a store as big as a large cathedral and an enormous refuge. It will not supply food and does not show on the Marauder's Map. In 1997 it stayed shut to repeated requests to see what another user was doing.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** Its inside is not tied to its outside: the same stretch of wall has opened on a broom cupboard, a room for 28 and a cathedral-sized store. Because: All three are reached through this one door place, and the store is far taller and larger than any space on the seventh floor could hold. (B05-111, B05-113, B06-156)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Its concealed entrance is on the seventh floor, in the blank wall facing the Barnabas tapestry. (B05-108, B06-125)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** When the last user leaves, the door melts back into stone at once. (B05-116, B06-155, B07-086)
- **Reported.** Harry has never seen it on the Marauder's Map, and Hermione suggests it is unplottable. (B06-127)
- **Explicit.** Closing its door shuts out outside sound completely: a battle fell silent in 1998. (B07-081)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** You walk past the wall three times thinking hard of what you need. A door then appears: highly polished with a brass handle on its first narrated use. (B05-109, B06-155, B07-081)
- **Reported.** Dobby says it can be entered only by someone in real need, comes and goes, and is equipped for what the seeker needs. Few know of it. (B05-105)
- **Explicit.** Asking in the wrong words fails: in 1997 repeated requests to see what Malfoy was doing left the wall doorless. (B06-126)
- **Reported.** A user can exclude others: the 1998 refuge stayed shut to the Carrows while one defender stayed inside, and Trelawney reports being thrown out in 1997. (B06-165, B07-050)

### What is inside

- **Reported.** Reported earlier forms include an elf-sized bed with Butterbeer antidotes, extra cleaning supplies and a well-proportioned room of chamber pots that was gone later. (B04-127, B05-106)
- **Reported.** It does not make food. After about a day and a half of hunger it opened a passage to the Hog's Head instead. (B07-050)

### How it changes

- **Reported.** It cannot change form while anyone remains inside, so it had to be emptied in 1998 before being asked for a new room. (B07-078)
- **Reported.** It can grow in use: the refuge expanded and added hammocks and a bathroom as people arrived. (B07-049)
- **Reported.** After the 1998 fire its door vanished, and Ron asks whether it still works. The books do not answer. (B07-086)

### Open questions

- Whether to model the Room as a true room in the castle or as a separate volume behind a door. The text supports a separate volume, but never says so.

## Room of Requirement: DA practice room

This is the Room of Requirement as Dumbledore's Army used it from October 1995 to spring 1996: a spacious stone-walled room lit by flickering torches, its walls lined with wooden bookcases holding hundreds of books, large silk cushions in place of chairs, and shelves of Dark detectors at the far end. It seats all 28 members on cushions and takes 14 duelling pairs, but it is too tight for everyone to practise Stunning at once. The door has a working key. Its contents shift from meeting to meeting.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is a spacious room seating all 28 members on large silk cushions, with room for them to duel in pairs. (B05-110, B05-111, B05-114)
- **Explicit.** It is too confined for all 28 to practise Stunning at once: half watch while half cast. (B05-146)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least about 85 m2. Because: Assumes 6 m2 for a duelling pair: 14 pairs x 6 m2 = 84 m2. The room copes with this, but not with 28 pupils Stunning onto cushions. (B05-111, B05-114, B05-146)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** It has stone walls and flickering torches like those of the dungeons eight floors below, and it can be brightly lit. (B05-110, B05-145, B05-197)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The door has a key standing in a working lock, and pupils rushing out formed a scrum at it. (B05-112, B05-198)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Wooden bookcases line the walls with hundreds of large leather-bound defence books. (B05-111)
- **Explicit.** Far-end shelves hold Dark detectors: Sneakoscopes, Secrecy Sensors and a big cracked Foe-Glass. A later table of Sneakoscopes was reduced to dust. (B05-112, B05-117)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** December 1995: a hundred golden baubles with Harry's face hung from the ceiling, with a large clump of mistletoe. (B05-145)
- **Reported.** From October 1995 the signed Dumbledore's Army list was pinned to a wall, until it was taken in spring 1996. (B05-114, B05-203)

### Open questions

- Room dimensions and ceiling height. This state is a dated requirement, not a permanent layout.

## Room of Requirement: Room of Hidden Things

The Room of Hidden Things is the Room of Requirement as a hiding place, seen in May 1997 and May 1998. It is as big as a large cathedral, with high windows sending shafts of light over what looks like a city: towering walls of hidden objects, alleyways and roads between them. Things have been left there for centuries, including thousands and thousands of books, broken furniture, weapons and broomsticks, and one wall of junk stands fifty feet high. A stuffed troll, a broken Vanishing Cabinet and a blistered cupboard mark one route in. Crabbe's cursed fire destroyed much of it in May 1998.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is as big as a large cathedral, a gigantic labyrinth, with towering walls of stored objects laid out like a city. (B06-156, B07-026, B07-082)
- **Explicit.** One wall of junk is fifty feet high, about 15 m. That is the heap, not the ceiling. (B07-082, B07-084)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 100 m by 30 m on plan and a ceiling well above 15 m. Because: A large cathedral is taken as at least 100 m long and 30 m wide (assumed). The ceiling must clear 15 m heaps with room for broom flight above them. (B06-156, B07-082, B07-085)
- **Inference.** It cannot physically sit behind a seventh-floor wall. Because: A height over 15 m is more than four storeys at 3.5 m, and no floor above the seventh is named. Treat the interior as a separate volume. (B05-108, B06-156, B07-082)
- **Explicit.** It holds thousands and thousands of books, among centuries of objects left by earlier inhabitants. (B06-157, B07-026, B07-071)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** One route in: right at an enormous stuffed troll, a short run, left at a broken Vanishing Cabinet, to a large blistered cupboard. (B06-158, B07-082)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** High windows send shafts of daylight down into it. (B06-156)
- **Explicit.** Alleyways, roads and aisles run between the piles, wide enough to run along and with walls high enough to hide people in the next aisle. (B06-156, B06-158, B07-082)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** It opens to a request for a hiding place or for where things are hidden. A summoning charm fails inside, and the huge space echoes. (B06-155, B06-165, B07-081)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Piles hold broken furniture, trunks, robes, crates, bottles of congealed potion, hats, jewels, cloaks, swords, a bloodstained axe, broomsticks, Fanged Frisbees and winged catapults, a few still hovering. (B06-157, B07-082)
- **Explicit.** The cupboard holds a cage with a five-legged skeleton. On it stood a chipped bust of an old warlock with a dusty wig and a tarnished tiara, placed there in 1997. (B06-159, B07-083)
- **Reported.** The Vanishing Cabinet is one of a pair, its mate in Borgin and Burkes. Repaired by 1997, it gave a route into the school. (B06-179)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** May 1998: Crabbe's spell made a fifty-foot wall collapse into the next aisle, and a curse blasted the bust. (B07-084)
- **Explicit.** May 1998: cursed fire swept the heaps to soot, forming fiery beasts and thick black smoke. The diadem was recovered and destroyed in the corridor outside. (B07-085, B07-086, B07-087)

### Open questions

- Whether anything survived the fire, and whether the Room can take this form again.

## Room of Requirement: resistance refuge

The refuge is the Room of Requirement as students' hideout in spring 1998. It began small, with one hammock and Gryffindor hangings, and grew until it was enormous, resembling a sumptuous tree house or a gigantic ship's cabin: windowless walls of dark wood panelling, a balcony running round them, multicoloured hammocks strung from ceiling and balcony, and bright House tapestries for all but Slytherin. Bookcases, broomsticks, lamps and a wireless furnish it, and a bathroom opens off it. A passage leads to the Hog's Head, and a cupboard in a corner opens on a long steep stair whose exit changes daily. Hundreds of evacuees passed through it on the night of the battle.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is enormous, like a sumptuous tree house or gigantic ship's cabin. More than twenty people crowded round Harry there on his arrival. (B07-047, B07-048)
- **Reported.** It is reported to have started much smaller, with one hammock, and to have grown over nearly two weeks as more people arrived. (B07-049)
- **Inference.** On the battle night it took hundreds: evacuees streamed to it and out through the Hog's Head passage, after it had been packed with fighters. Because: Harry's map shows a dense crowd heading there, he hears hundreds marching towards it, and Aberforth reports hundreds through his pub. (B07-064, B07-069)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The walls are dark wood panelling without windows, with a balcony running round them. (B07-047)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A short flight of steps and a small door at the far end of the Hog's Head passage open into the room. (B07-044, B07-050)
- **Explicit.** A small corner cupboard opens on a steep, long torch-lit stair with unexpected bends, ending at a wall that melts to a push and reseals at once. (B07-051, B07-064)
- **Explicit.** Neville says the stair's outlet moves each day. On the night of the battle, May 1998, it came out on the fifth floor. (B07-051)
- **Reported.** While one defender stays inside, the door will not open to the Carrows or their supporters. (B07-050)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Multicoloured hammocks hang from ceiling and balcony, and it makes more as needed. (B07-047, B07-049)
- **Explicit.** Bright tapestries show the Gryffindor lion on scarlet, the Hufflepuff badger on yellow and the Ravenclaw eagle on blue. Slytherin's are absent. (B07-047)
- **Explicit.** Bulging bookcases, broomsticks against the walls, lamps, chairs and a large wooden-cased wireless in a corner furnish it. (B07-047, B07-048)
- **Explicit.** A bathroom opens directly off the room by an open doorway. It is reported to have appeared when girls arrived. (B07-049, B07-064)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Mrs Longbottom sealed the Hog's Head passage behind her, and the last occupants left by the stone steps so the Room could be asked for something else. (B07-078)

### Conflicts

- The Room's door is on the seventh floor, yet the refuge stair runs up to a fifth-floor corridor, which puts this state below the fifth floor that night. Neville's moving outlet explains the exit but not the depth. (B05-108, B07-051, B07-064)

### Open questions

- Size and height of the room at its largest, and where the seventh-floor door opens into it.
