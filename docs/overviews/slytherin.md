# Slytherin house spaces

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/slytherin.md)

Slytherin's rooms are underground. Coming down the marble staircase, a door on the right leads to steps and a passage, then to a bare, damp length of stone wall where a concealed stone door slides open to a password. Inside is a long, low common room of undressed stone, with round greenish lamps on chains, a fire under a carved mantelpiece and carved chairs. Harry later says it lies under the lake with green light, though no window is described. The dormitories are also in the dungeons, close by, and have never been seen.

## Where it is

- **Explicit.** Common room and dormitories both lie in the dungeons, below the Entrance Hall. (B02-093, B04-122, B06-124)

## Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The common room is long and low, of undressed stone, lit by round greenish lamps on chains and a fire under a carved mantelpiece. (B02-093)
- **Reported.** Harry says it is under the lake, which turns the light green. This is his unconfirmed account. (B07-028)

## Getting there

- **Explicit.** The way runs through a door on the right as you come down the marble staircase, down steps, along a passage, to a concealed stone door that obeys a password. (B02-092, B02-095, B05-302)

## Open questions

- Whether lake windows exist, and how the dormitories connect to the common room.

## Slytherin common room

The Slytherin common room is underground in the dungeons. Coming down the marble staircase, a door on the right leads to stone steps and a passage. The entrance is a concealed stone door in a bare, damp length of wall, which opens to a password. Inside is a long, low room with undressed stone walls and ceiling, round greenish lamps hanging on chains, a fire under an elaborately carved mantelpiece and carved chairs. Harry later says it lies under the lake and that the light is green, but no window is ever described.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** Size it for about 200 people: at least about 150 m2. Because: Two hundred people wore Slytherin green at the 1994 final. If the room takes the whole House as Gryffindor's does, 200 x 0.75 m2 = 150 m2. The per-person area is assumed. (B02-093, B03-106)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies underground in the dungeons, below the Entrance Hall. (B02-093, B04-122, B06-129)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** It is a long, low room under the ground, walled and ceiled in undressed stone, long enough for two boys to sprint its length. (B02-093, B02-095)
- **Reported.** Harry tells captors it is under the lake, full of skulls, and that the light is green. His claim was accepted as convincing. (B07-028)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** Coming down the marble staircase, a door on the right leads down steps to a passage and on to the common-room wall. (B02-095, B04-122, B05-302)
- **Explicit.** The entrance is a stone door concealed in bare damp wall, sliding open to a password: Pure-blood at Christmas 1992. No handle or portrait marks it. (B02-092)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Round greenish lamps hang on chains from the ceiling. (B02-093)
- **Explicit.** A fire burns under an elaborately carved mantelpiece straight ahead of the entrance, with carved chairs round it and more chairs set back. (B02-093)

### Open questions

- Whether it has windows into the lake. The one narrated visit mentions only lamps.
- Where the doors to the dormitories are.

## Slytherin dormitories

The Slytherin dormitories are in the dungeons, reported by a house-elf and shown on the Marauder's Map, and close enough to the common room for a return trip in about a minute. They are never described.

### Where it is

- **Reported.** They lie in the dungeons, as Kreacher reports of Draco Malfoy's dormitory in 1997. (B06-124)
- **Inference.** They are close to the common room: Malfoy fetched a cutting and came back about a minute later. Because: He leaves to get it and returns a minute later. He may have kept it elsewhere, but no other place is implied. (B02-093)
- **Explicit.** The Marauder's Map shows a Slytherin dormitory. (B06-107)
