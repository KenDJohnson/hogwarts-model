Ravenclaw's rooms are in a tower in the west of the castle, one of the three highest, and not the West Tower. A spiral stair climbs in tight, dizzying circles to a handleless old door with a bronze eagle knocker, which asks a question and opens to a reasoned answer. Inside is a wide, circular, airy common room with arched windows and silk hangings of blue and bronze, a domed star-painted ceiling and midnight-blue carpet, with tables, chairs and glass-fronted bookcases. Opposite the door a niche holds a tall marble statue of Rowena Ravenclaw. A door beside it leads up a stair to the dormitories above.

## Ravenclaw Tower

Ravenclaw Tower stands in the west of the castle and is named with Astronomy and Gryffindor as one of the three highest towers. It is not the West Tower. A spiral stair, reached on foot from the fifth floor in the one narrated visit, climbs in tight, dizzying circles to a door of plain old wood with no handle or keyhole, only a bronze eagle knocker. The eagle asks a question and the door opens to a well-reasoned answer, not a password. Behind the door lies the common room, with dormitories above it, so the tower must rise well beyond its entrance. Its floor numbers are never given.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** The tower holds the common room with dormitories above it, so a House's dormitories rise beyond the door. Because: Footsteps of sleepers above shake the ceiling, and a stair behind a door by the statue leads to their beds. (B07-055, B07-056)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The Marauder's Map shows Ravenclaws going to a tower in the west of the castle. (B05-115, B06-129)
- **Reported.** It is one of the three highest towers, with Astronomy and Gryffindor, according to the 1998 battle plan. (B07-068)
- **Inference.** It stands below Astronomy and level in rank with Gryffindor. Neither is stated to be the taller, and it must overtop the West and North Towers. Because: Astronomy is the highest tower. Kingsley's list is no ranking, since it puts Astronomy second. (B06-175, B07-068)
- **Inference.** It is not the West Tower, despite standing in the west. Because: Harry often climbs to the Owlery at the top of West Tower, yet has never been up this stair. Only this one is among the three highest. (B04-034, B07-052)
- **Explicit.** In May 1998 Harry reached the stair's foot on foot from a fifth-floor corridor. Coming back, the party descended two more floors after the stair and corridors. (B07-052, B07-060)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A spiral stair climbs in tight, dizzying circles to the entrance door. (B07-052, B07-060)
- **Explicit.** The door is plain old wood with no handle or keyhole, only a bronze knocker shaped like an eagle. (B07-053)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A knock makes the eagle's beak ask a question in a musical voice. A reasoned answer opens the door; after a wrong answer you wait for someone who gets it right. (B07-053, B07-057)
- **Explicit.** Force and noise fail: shouting, shaking and loud bangs did not open it in 1998. A teacher from another House got in by answering, and Flitwick had earlier admitted Alecto Carrow. (B07-057)

### Open questions

- The floor of the stair's foot, the floor of the door, and the tower's plan and diameter. If it mirrors Gryffindor, about seven dormitory levels stand above the common room.

## Ravenclaw common room

The Ravenclaw common room is a wide circular room in Ravenclaw Tower, below the dormitories, and the airiest room Harry has seen at Hogwarts. Graceful arched windows break walls with blue and bronze silk hangings, and by day they show the surrounding mountains. The domed ceiling is painted with stars, echoed by a midnight-blue carpet. Tables, chairs and glass-fronted bookcases furnish it. Opposite the entrance a niche holds a tall white marble statue of Rowena Ravenclaw on a plinth, wearing a carved circlet. Beside the statue a door opens on the stair to the dormitories above.
