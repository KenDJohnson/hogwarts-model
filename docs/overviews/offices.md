# Offices, staff room and caretaker spaces

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/offices.md)

Staff offices are scattered through the castle, not grouped, and a teacher's office is often far from the classroom. From the bottom up: Snape's office is in the dungeons, close to Potions. Filch's windowless office is probably on the ground floor, as is the long panelled staff room. McGonagall's small office is on the first floor. Slughorn's oversized room lies between the marble staircase and the hospital wing. The Defence teacher's office is on the third floor, though Book 2 once says the second and a Book 4 route reads as the fourth. Flitwick's office is on the seventh floor, and the Headmaster's round office stands in a tower above a seventh-floor corridor. Trelawney lives at the top of North Tower. Madam Hooch's office is unplaced. The seventh floor alone carries Flitwick's and the Headmaster's rooms, the Gryffindor entrance, the Room of Requirement and the foot of the North Tower stair, so it must be a full, long storey.

## Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The text puts on the seventh floor: Fat Lady's portrait, Room of Requirement wall and Barnabas tapestry, Cadogan's landing, North Tower stair, Flitwick's office, Head's gargoyle, Lachlan statue and a boys' bathroom. (B01-046, B03-089, B03-111, B03-139, B05-059, B05-108, B05-199, B06-035, B06-125)
- **Inference.** One level meets four towers: Gryffindor's above its entrance, North Tower, the Head's tower and West Tower, whose flank is counted window by window on this floor. Because: Each connection is on or read from the seventh floor. They need not be close together, and Harry places two of the entrances on far sides of the castle. (B01-046, B03-111, B03-139, B06-035)
- **Inference.** The seventh floor must be a full storey running round the castle, not a small top gallery. Because: The Room and the gargoyle are round opposite sides of the castle yet linked on foot on this level, and Flitwick's window is thirteen bays from the West Tower. (B03-139, B06-128, B06-167)
- **Inference.** Capacity shown: Defence office fourteen people, Headmaster's nine, McGonagall's eight, Snape's five. Because: Head counts from named people present in single scenes. Each is a minimum, not a maximum. (B02-012, B02-158, B05-201, B05-276)

## Where it is

- **Explicit.** Teachers' offices need not adjoin their classrooms: Flitwick's is on the seventh floor while Charms is lower, and from 1996 Snape teaches floors above his dungeon office. (B03-139, B04-084, B06-160)

## Conflicts

- The hospital wing is the hinge of several floor problems. Book 5 puts it on the third floor. Yet Pomfrey is asked to come down from it to the Defence office, also on the third, and the Headmaster's office, above a seventh-floor corridor, is one flight above it. (B05-268, B04-233, B04-234)

## Open questions

- Decide the hospital wing's floor before fixing the Defence office and the gargoyle, because their stated routes all run through it.

## Headmaster’s office

The Headmaster's office is a round room at the top of a tower, entered from a corridor on the seventh floor. A lone stone gargoyle guards the way: a spoken password moves it aside, the wall behind opens, and a stone spiral stair carries visitors up by itself to an oak door with a griffin knocker. The text calls the room large, yet eight people fill it. It holds an enormous clawed desk, a fireplace on the Floo network, tables of whirring silver instruments, a black cabinet for the Pensieve, a shelf for the Sorting Hat, a glass case for Gryffindor's sword and a golden perch by the door. Portraits of past heads cover the walls almost to the ceiling. Several windows look down over the grounds as far as the Quidditch stadium, and between them they catch both sunrise and sunset. The same room serves every head from Dippet onward. Book 6 fixes the floor, but Books 2 and 4 describe approaches that read as much lower; see the conflicts.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** The office floor stands at least one storey above the corridor, and possibly more. Because: The moving stair makes repeated circles and leaves a first-time rider dizzy (B02-C011-P0196, B05-C022-P0005). No rise is given. (B02-083, B05-150)
- **Explicit.** The text calls the room large and people walk around it, yet at a 1996 hearing eight people make it read as full; a ninth then joins them. (B02-084, B04-200, B05-201, B05-288)
- **Explicit.** A small rock island in a distant cave is measured against it: no bigger than this office. (B06-172)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 8 m across inside. Because: A circle 8 m wide has 50 sq m. Assumed: 10 for the enormous desk and chairs, 10 for tables, cabinet, hearth and perch, 9 for nine standing people, 20 for circulation. (B02-084, B05-201)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** A lone stone gargoyle against a seventh-floor corridor wall hides the entrance; the office is a round room at the top of a tower above that corridor. (B06-035, B06-167, B07-012)
- **Explicit.** The gargoyle stands round a corner, halfway along an otherwise empty stretch of corridor; after a year at school, pupils still do not know where it is and see no sign to it. (B02-083, B04-188, B06-167, B01-150)
- **Explicit.** From the Fat Lady's corridor it takes a few minutes on foot, and from North Tower a walk along corridors; no stairs are mentioned on either route. (B05-149, B06-216, B04-198)
- **Reported.** Harry tells a visitor it lies round the far side of the castle from the Room of Requirement, yet in 1997 he walks from one towards the other with no stairs mentioned. (B06-128, B06-167, B07-071)
- **Explicit.** More than one window looks down across the grounds, as far as the distant Quidditch stadium; one has curtains in an old memory, and an outer ledge collects snow. (B02-104, B05-291, B06-064, B06-122, B06-167, B06-199, B07-114)
- **Inference.** Between them the windows see dawn over mountains and also sunset, so they face roughly opposite ways. Because: Sunrise over mountains is seen through a window in B05-C037; sunset is seen through a window in B02-C013 and B06-C025. (B02-104, B05-288, B06-167)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A spoken password makes the gargoyle jump aside and the wall behind it part; the wall shuts again behind those who enter. (B02-083, B04-189, B05-150, B06-035)
- **Explicit.** Passwords used: Sherbet lemon 1992, Cockroach cluster 1995, Fizzing Whizzbee 1995-96, Acid Pops and Toffee eclairs 1996-97. In 1998 the word Dumbledore works, then the displaced gargoyle simply lets people pass. (B02-083, B04-199, B05-150, B05-200, B06-030, B06-119, B07-104, B07-133)
- **Explicit.** Behind the wall a stone spiral stair rises by itself in tight circles to a polished oak door bearing a griffin-shaped brass knocker; it can also be climbed on foot. (B02-083, B05-150, B06-119, B07-133)
- **Explicit.** Besides the stair, the office has been entered or left by Floo through its fireplace, by Portkey and by phoenix. (B05-156, B05-205, B05-290, B05-297)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** An enormous claw-footed polished desk has a high-backed chair behind it, visitor seating opposite and a rug in front. The room also has a fireplace, lamps, a clock and carpet. (B02-084, B04-200, B05-201, B06-039, B06-095, B06-119, B07-105, B07-113, B05-293, B06-006)
- **Explicit.** Spindle-legged tables around the room carry delicate silver instruments that whirr and give off puffs of smoke. (B02-084, B05-151, B05-154, B06-036)
- **Explicit.** Portraits of past heads fill the walls all round, some as high as the ceiling; named subjects include Dippet, Fortescue, Phineas Nigellus, Everard and Dilys Derwent. (B02-084, B05-152, B05-155, B05-201, B06-120, B06-198, B07-105, B07-134, B06-063, B06-148, B07-013, B07-115)
- **Explicit.** From June 1997 Dumbledore's portrait, the largest, hangs directly behind the head's chair and swings forward from a hidden wall cavity. (B06-197, B07-105, B07-116, B07-134)
- **Explicit.** A shelf behind the desk holds the Sorting Hat; a wall-mounted glass case beside it holds Gryffindor's ruby-hilted sword, with Phineas's portrait next to the case. (B04-201, B06-096, B06-149, B05-020, B05-084, B07-018)
- **Explicit.** Near the door stand the golden perch, with an ash tray beneath it, and a black cabinet with a sliding catch for the shallow stone Pensieve. Another cupboard once held a kettle. (B02-085, B04-202, B05-156, B05-290, B05-294, B06-037, B06-062)
- **Reported.** Hermione says Dumbledore kept books taken from the library in his study, and that they later flew out of its window; his private library passes to the school in 1997. (B07-004, B07-005, B07-007)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** March 1996: a fight overturns the desk, breaks instruments and tables and fills the room with dust. By June it is whole again, until Harry smashes more instruments. (B05-204, B05-288, B05-292)
- **Reported.** The room passes from Dippet to Dumbledore, McGonagall and Snape. In 1996 it reportedly sealed itself against Umbridge, and Phineas says it admits only the rightful head. (B02-104, B05-207, B05-289, B06-197, B07-012)
- **Reported.** 1997: pupils reportedly smash the sword case, and the displayed sword is sent to Gringotts. (B07-017)
- **Explicit.** May 1998: the gargoyle is knocked lopsided but still speaks, and the portraits empty and later refill. (B07-105, B07-133, B07-134)

### Conflicts

- Floor of the gargoyle. Book 6 says a seventh-floor corridor, and Book 5 makes it minutes from the Fat Lady. In Book 4 Harry takes the marble stairs, making for the second floor, and is at it five minutes later. (B06-035, B05-149, B04-188)
- Books 2 and 4 read as well below the seventh floor. In 1992 it is one corner from a corridor whose victims are taken up to the hospital wing. In 1995 the office is one flight of stairs above that wing, which Book 5 puts two floors above the top of the marble staircase, the third floor. (B02-082, B02-083, B04-234, B05-268)
- In 1995 Dumbledore says he will take Harry upstairs from the Defence office, but the narration gives only a dark passage before the gargoyle, which reads as a short walk, not four floors of climbing. Harry's 1992 run round the whole second floor, by contrast, mentions no gargoyle. (B04-233, B02-046)

### Open questions

- Place the gargoyle on the seventh floor: it is the only passage that names the corridor's own floor, and it fits Book 5. Books 2 and 4 stay unreconciled, and the hospital wing cannot then be one flight below the office.
- How far the stair rises, and how far the tower stands above the seventh floor, is not given.

## McGonagall’s office

McGonagall's office, also called her study, is a small room on the first floor, reached from the Entrance Hall by the marble staircase and a corridor. It has a large fire in a grate that can join the Floo network, windows that rattle in storms and look out to the Quidditch pitch, a desk with drawers, visitor chairs, a carpet and a tin of biscuits. The first-floor statement comes from Book 1, and everything later fits it except one short walk from Myrtle's bathroom. From the office the hospital wing and Gryffindor Tower are up, and the Great Hall is down. When lessons end, hundreds of pupils can be heard overhead.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is called a small room, with a large fire. (B03-010)
- **Inference.** Small as it is, it holds at least eight people at once. Because: May 1993: Mr and Mrs Weasley, Dumbledore, McGonagall, Harry, Ron, Ginny and Lockhart (B02-C018-P0001 to P0004). (B02-158)
- **Explicit.** When the bell rings, hundreds of pupils can be heard moving overhead and all around. (B05-044)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It is on the first floor. (B01-103)
- **Explicit.** From the Entrance Hall the way is the marble staircase, then a corridor. (B03-010, B05-125, B05-302, B06-058)
- **Explicit.** It is upstairs from ground-floor classroom eleven. (B05-229)
- **Reported.** The twins say they must take friends down to it from the library. (B04-155)
- **Explicit.** From the office the hospital wing is up, the Great Hall is down, and more stairs lead on to the Gryffindor common room. (B02-159, B06-058, B06-060)
- **Explicit.** A corridor outside leads to a stair head with a lower landing and a moonlit window; a corridor on the way to Gryffindor overlooks Hagrid's cabin. (B02-160, B06-090)
- **Explicit.** Her window shows the Quidditch pitch. (B03-048)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Windows rattle in their frames in sleet, and the room can stay chilly despite the fire. (B06-059)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The door shuts by itself behind a visitor, and voices carry along the corridor. (B05-044, B05-229)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A fire in a grate with a mantelpiece has chairs beside it; ash on the carpet is a concern when pupils arrive through it. (B02-158, B06-089)
- **Explicit.** A desk with drawers faces a visitor's chair and carries piles of papers, pamphlets and a tartan tin of Ginger Newts. (B02-158, B05-044, B05-126, B05-229)
- **Reported.** She says she is used to keeping the Quidditch Cup in her study. (B05-119)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** January 1997: a one-off Floo link brings pupils back through this fireplace. (B06-089)
- **Reported.** February 1995: the second-task hostages are reportedly put into an enchanted sleep here. (B04-171)

### Conflicts

- First floor in Book 1, but in 1993 the party leaves Myrtle's bathroom and reaches the office moments later along a corridor, with no stairs mentioned. The bathroom is on the second-floor corridor; Hermione once says first floor, which would fit, but Book 4 says second. (B01-103, B02-157, B02-045, B04-115)

## Snape’s office and private stores

Snape's office is a stone-floored room in the dungeons, behind a door halfway along a cold passage reached by a narrow stone stair from the Entrance Hall. He keeps it even after moving to teach Defence in 1996. Shelves of hundreds of glass jars holding preserved creatures line the dim walls. It has a desk with a chair opposite, two chairs by a fireplace that takes Floo travel, a corner cupboard of private ingredients, a table and a large wall clock. Crowd noise from the grounds does not reach it, though a scream from the Entrance Hall does.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** Shelves carry hundreds of glass jars of animal and plant parts in coloured potions. (B02-012, B05-164, B06-161)
- **Explicit.** Five people meet here in September 1992; in 1996 a staggering pupil covers several steps before hitting the shelves. (B02-012, B05-185)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It lies in the dungeons, down a narrow stone stair from the Entrance Hall, one storey below the ground floor. Because: The stair and dungeon location are explicit. The dungeons are eight floors below the seventh floor (B05-C018-P0190), which makes them one storey down. (B02-011, B05-186, B06-088, B05-110)
- **Explicit.** Its door is halfway along a cold passage. (B02-011)
- **Inference.** It must be close to the Potions classroom. Because: In 1992 Hermione slips out of a lesson in the confusion, takes ingredients from his stores and is back in time to hide them under her robes (B02-C011-P0040 to P0044). (B02-074)
- **Explicit.** The Marauder's Map shows it in the bottom left-hand corner. (B04-139)
- **Explicit.** Walking there from the steps leaves crowd noise from the grounds behind entirely; a scream from the Entrance Hall still reaches it. (B05-186, B06-160)
- **Explicit.** Fleeing it in 1996, Harry stops only after putting three floors between himself and Snape. (B05-222)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The floor is stone and the room is dim, lit by firelight, candles or torches. (B03-097, B04-149, B05-164, B05-222)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** Snape says the office is sealed by a spell only a wizard can break, yet stores vanish from it in 1992-95. (B04-149, B04-174, B04-230)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A desk with a chair opposite, two chairs by the fire, a table and a large wall clock. (B02-012, B05-164, B06-161)
- **Explicit.** The fireplace works for Floo travel, with powder in a jar nearby; Lupin arrives through it in 1994. (B03-097)
- **Explicit.** A corner cupboard holds his private ingredients, separate from the classroom store. (B04-174, B05-164)
- **Explicit.** Specimens named: a large slimy thing in green liquid behind the desk, a dead frog in purple liquid, an eel, and a jar of cockroaches. (B02-012, B05-185, B05-222)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** 1996: the Pensieve stands on the desk or a shelf during Occlumency lessons. (B05-165, B05-218)
- **Explicit.** May 1997: cobwebbed boxes of old punishment cards pile up on the table. (B06-161)
- **Explicit.** From 1996 he teaches floors above but does not give up this room. (B06-160)

## Defence teacher’s office

The Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher's office is one room that the text says served Lockhart, Lupin, the false Moody and Umbridge in turn, with only the contents changing. Its last stated floor is the third, though Book 2 once sends Harry along the second-floor corridor to it and a Book 4 route reads as the fourth. It is a separate room from the Defence classroom, reached from the marble staircase by its own corridor, which has a large suit of armour and a recess with a stone bust. The room has one lockable door, a window with a sill, a distant view of the Quidditch goalposts and the grounds, a stone floor, a hearth on the Floo network and a desk with drawers. It is a fair size: fourteen people stood in it at once in 1996, and a very large tank fitted in one corner. From 1996 Snape keeps his dungeon office, and the text never returns to this room.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** It holds at least fourteen people at once. Because: June 1996: Umbridge, Harry, Hermione, Malfoy, Millicent Bulstrode, four captives, four captors, then Snape (B05-C032-P0158 to P0171). (B05-276)
- **Explicit.** In 1992 eight people gather around the desk, some in chairs; in 1993 a very large water tank fits in one corner. (B02-048, B03-037, B07-001)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The latest explicit statement, in 1995, puts the office on the third floor. (B05-055)
- **Inference.** Book 2's routes agree with the third floor, one level above the second-floor attack corridor. Because: Lockhart calls the office just upstairs from that corridor (B02-C009-P0010), and pupils march him down the nearest stairs to it (B02-C016-P0169). (B02-048, B02-138)
- **Explicit.** An empty classroom lies one floor above it. (B02-049)
- **Explicit.** The way in goes up the marble staircase, past its first landing, then along a corridor; leaving, one landing down is still above the marble staircase. (B04-224, B05-210, B05-232, B05-278)
- **Inference.** It may lie near the Charms corridor. Because: In 1994 Moody leaves a classroom beside that corridor and walks Harry to the office with no stairs mentioned (B04-C020-P0038 to P0043). (B04-084)
- **Explicit.** Its corridor has a large suit of armour, a recess with a muttering stone bust on a column, and another way round by a swivelling staircase. (B05-230, B05-271, B05-273)
- **Explicit.** From the window the Quidditch pitch is visible far off: goalposts and play can be followed until dark, but individual players are hard to make out. (B05-058)
- **Reported.** The window also overlooks the grounds; pupils say they saw two friends crossing towards the Forest from it. (B05-274, B05-284)
- **Explicit.** Screams from the distant pitch can be heard inside on a June night. (B04-225)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The text states that this one room served Lockhart, Lupin, the false Moody and Umbridge, and speaks of teachers living in it. (B04-085, B05-055)
- **Explicit.** One door with a key lock opens on the corridor. A window with a sill opens to the outside. The floor is stone, carpeted by 1996. (B02-137, B04-225, B05-231, B05-276)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** Umbridge says hers is the one unwatched fire in the school, that sensing spells guard her doorway, and that the office has a mirror. (B05-213, B05-275, B05-202)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A desk with drawers stays through every occupant; a hearth has a grate, stacked logs and a small box of Floo powder. (B02-137, B03-161, B05-056, B05-231, B05-232)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** 1992-93 Lockhart: many candles and walls covered in countless framed photographs of himself, with a life-size poster behind the door. (B02-033, B02-137, B04-085)
- **Explicit.** 1993-94 Lupin: kettle, tea things and Dark creatures kept for lessons in cages or tanks. (B03-037, B03-161, B05-055)
- **Reported.** Lupin says he would keep a Boggart in a cupboard under his desk and spend full-moon nights curled up in the office. (B03-078, B03-127)
- **Explicit.** 1994-95 false Moody: Sneakoscope on the desk, Secrecy Sensor on a corner table, Foe-Glass on the wall, and a large trunk with seven keyholes under the window. (B04-086, B04-226)
- **Reported.** Moody says his Sneakoscope senses trouble about a mile off and that figures in the Foe-Glass sharpen as enemies near. (B04-087)
- **Explicit.** The trunk's seventh lock opens on a pit-like space ten feet deep holding the real Moody. It is inside the trunk, not a cellar. (B04-227)
- **Explicit.** June 1995: Dumbledore blasts the door to splinters. (B04-225)
- **Explicit.** 1995-96 Umbridge: lace cloths, doilies, vases of dried flowers, plates of moving kittens, a chintz armchair, a small table and a block reading HEADMISTRESS. (B05-056, B05-211, B05-276, B07-014)
- **Explicit.** Spring 1996: brooms chained to an iron peg behind the desk burst through the door, which Filch replaces; Nifflers then wreck the room, put in by the window. (B05-211, B05-234, B05-236, B05-237, B05-266, B05-127)
- **Inference.** The text is silent on this room after 1996: Snape keeps his dungeon office while teaching Defence. Because: B06-C024-P0144 says he did not vacate the dungeon office; no later scene uses this room. (B06-160)

### Conflicts

- Second or third floor. In 1992 Harry walks the second-floor corridor to the office, but in 1995 it is on the third floor, and Book 2's own routes put it one level above the second-floor attack corridor. (B02-033, B02-048, B02-138, B05-055)
- Book 5 pulls both ways. Noise is heard several floors below the office, which needs a high floor; yet one floor down fireworks fill the corridors and some escape towards the second floor, which suits an office on the second or fourth floor, not the third. (B05-214)
- A fourth-floor reading also exists. In January 1995 Moody and Harry climb the shortcut stair to its top, one floor below the fifth-floor Prefects' bathroom, and walk straight to the office door. Moody's 'up to my office' from the third-floor classroom and the Book 5 fireworks both fit it. (B04-131, B04-147, B04-150, B04-031, B05-214)
- In 1995 Pomfrey is asked to come down to this office from the hospital wing, so the wing is higher. Book 5 puts the wing on the third floor, which would make the office lower than third. (B04-233, B05-268)

### Open questions

- Use the third floor: it is the latest explicit statement and Book 2's routes support it. The single second-floor line and the fourth-floor hints stay unexplained.
- The text never says where Quirrell or the Defence teachers of 1996-98 had offices, so do not assume this room before 1992 or after 1996.

## Filch’s office

Filch's office is a dingy, windowless room with a low ceiling, directly below a classroom. A single oil lamp hangs from the ceiling, filing cabinets stand around the walls, and polished chains and manacles hang behind the desk. No floor is stated. Book 2 puts it right under the room where the Vanishing Cabinet crashed, and Book 5 puts that cabinet on the first floor, which suggests the ground floor.

### Size and numbers

- **Reported.** Dumbledore says the list of banned items, about 437 by 1994, can be read here, and by 1995 it is fastened to the office door. (B04-017, B05-022)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** A classroom lies directly above: a cabinet crashed there shakes the lamp. (B02-039)
- **Inference.** It is probably on the ground floor. Because: The wrecked cabinet lay right over the office (B02-C008-P0061, P0062). Book 5 places a Vanishing Cabinet on the first floor (B05-C028-P0025), and Book 6 calls it broken and long unused. Assumes one cabinet, unmoved. (B02-039, B05-209, B06-179)
- **Explicit.** It is downstairs from a tapestried corridor with a window; a corridor leads from it to stairs going back up. (B02-037)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The room is dingy and windowless with a low ceiling, lit by one oil lamp hanging from it, and it smells faintly of fried fish. (B02-038)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Wooden filing cabinets stand around the walls, with folders under pupils' names and a whole drawer for the Weasley twins. (B02-038, B07-015)
- **Explicit.** Polished chains and manacles hang on the wall behind the desk; the desk has drawers, a quill pot and a moth-eaten chair beside it. (B02-038)
- **Reported.** A cabinet drawer labelled for dangerous confiscations once held the Marauder's Map; Filch says he keeps the chains oiled. (B01-106, B03-060)

### Open questions

- The floor is inferred from one cabinet remark. If that cabinet moved between 1992 and 1996, the office could be on another floor.

## Flitwick’s office

Flitwick's office is a room on the seventh floor, far above his Charms classroom. Its window is the thirteenth to the right of the West Tower, and it can be picked out from the lake shore. In June 1994 it served as a locked cell.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** The window lies at least about 40 m along the facade from the West Tower. Because: Thirteen windows at an assumed 3 m spacing is 39 m. A modeller should not squeeze thirteen window bays into a short wall. (B03-139)

### Where it is

- **Reported.** Dumbledore puts it on the seventh floor, at the thirteenth window to the right of the West Tower; Pomfrey says the prisoner is upstairs from the hospital wing. (B03-139)
- **Inference.** The window faces the lake side of the castle, to the right of the West Tower as seen from the shore. Because: From the lake shore Harry counts the windows to the right of the tower and then flies straight there (B03-C021-P0315, P0323). If Ron is right that the lake is south, this facade faces south with the West Tower at its west end, which suits the tower's name. (B03-152, B03-153)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The locked glazed window opens just wide enough for a thin adult to squeeze out; the room has a chair and a door that locks. The text gives no room size. (B03-154)

## Slughorn’s office

Slughorn's office from 1996 is a room up the marble staircase, on a corridor of classrooms and below the hospital wing; no floor is stated. It is much larger than an ordinary teacher's study, whether by building or by magic. For the Christmas party of 1996 it is hung like a tent and packed with guests. In everyday use it is overheated and crowded with armchairs, low tables and a drinks table.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is far bigger than an ordinary teacher's study; the text leaves open whether it was built so or magically enlarged. (B06-085)
- **Explicit.** At the party it is crowded and stuffy, yet singing comes from a distant corner. No head count is given. (B06-086)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It is reached by going up the marble staircase from the Entrance Hall. (B06-084)
- **Reported.** Harry says Ron was brought up from here to the hospital wing, so the wing is higher. (B06-111)
- **Inference.** It is probably on the first or second floor. Because: It is above the Entrance Hall and below the hospital wing, which Book 5 puts two floors above the top of the marble staircase, the third floor. (B05-268, B06-084, B06-111)
- **Explicit.** It opens on a corridor of classroom doors, with one last classroom beyond it and a distant corner. (B06-087)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Everyday contents: armchairs, a tasselled footstool, a drinks table, a low table and a potion kit, in a very warm room; a gramophone is heard, not seen. (B06-109, B06-131)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** December 1996: emerald, crimson and gold hangings cover walls and ceiling like a vast tent, and a golden lamp of live fairies hangs at the centre. (B06-085, B06-086)

## Madam Hooch’s office

Madam Hooch has an office that can be locked, where the Bludgers are kept between practices. Nothing else is known of it.

### What is inside

- **Reported.** Wood says the Bludgers have been locked in her office since the last practice. (B02-064)

### Open questions

- Floor, size and whether it is in the castle or near the pitch are all unknown.

## Staff room

The staff room is a long, large, panelled room shared by the teachers, on the ground floor by inference. Its door, flanked by two talking stone gargoyles by 1995, opens on a corridor that pupils use to reach a courtyard. Dark wooden chairs, old and mismatched, fill it, with a fire, a low armchair and, at the far end, an old wardrobe for spare robes. A class of pupils can stand around its walls with space to spare.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is described as large and long. (B02-134, B03-029)
- **Inference.** It holds the teaching staff at an emergency meeting, or a whole class standing against its walls with a clear stretch before the wardrobe. Because: All teachers are summoned here in 1993, and in 1993 a Defence class backs against the walls, leaving one pupil alone by the wardrobe (B03-C007-P0145). (B02-135, B03-029, B03-030)
- **Explicit.** From inside, hundreds of people are heard moving overhead during the 1993 evacuation. (B02-135)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It is on the ground floor. Because: The corridor outside carries a crowd straight out into a courtyard (B05-C017-P0104), and Snape heads for it from the Entrance Hall (B01-C016-P0073). (B01-121, B05-090, B05-091)
- **Explicit.** Pupils go down to it from the common room, the hospital wing and the History of Magic corridor. (B01-064, B02-134, B05-090)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The walls are panelled, and the door can be shut. (B02-134, B03-029)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** By 1995 two stone gargoyles flank the door and talk to callers; teachers open it from inside. The door also opens to a push. (B01-064, B05-091)
- **Explicit.** In 1993 Lupin's class reaches it from the Defence classroom by two corridors, past a broom cupboard. (B03-028)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Dark wooden chairs, old and mismatched, fill the room, including a low armchair. (B02-134, B03-029)
- **Explicit.** A fire burns here; the tale of Binns's death puts an armchair in front of it. (B01-030, B02-052)
- **Explicit.** An ugly old wardrobe of teachers' spare robes stands at the far end, close enough to bang against the wall. (B02-134, B03-029)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** May 1998: both gargoyles are smashed by a jinx through a broken window; their fragments still stir and speak. (B07-073)

### Conflicts

- The ground-floor reading clashes with Lupin's route: his class walks here from the Defence classroom, implied to be on the third floor, by two corridors with no stairs mentioned. (B03-028, B05-091, B06-027)

### Open questions

- No window is described in the room.
