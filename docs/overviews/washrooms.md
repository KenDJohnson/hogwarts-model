# Bathrooms, toilets and washing spaces

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/washrooms.md)

Bathrooms and toilets on many floors, listed from the top. The seventh floor has a boys' bathroom near the Room of Requirement. The sixth has a tiled boys' bathroom, wrecked in a 1997 duel. The fifth has the Prefects' bathroom, a white marble room with a sunken bath the size of a swimming pool and about a hundred golden taps. The fourth has a toilet where Montague was found. The second has several bathrooms, including, by most evidence, Moaning Myrtle's out-of-order toilet with the hidden entrance to the Chamber of Secrets, though one character puts that on the first floor. The girls' toilets of the 1991 troll are upstairs on an unstated floor. Elsewhere there are a girls' bathroom one floor below Transfiguration and other unlocated toilets. Myrtle moves between them by the pipes.

## Size and numbers

- **Inference.** Bathrooms are spread over at least five floors, from the second to the seventh. Because: Named examples on the second (B05-239), fourth (B05-209), fifth (B04-131), sixth (B06-152) and seventh (B05-199) floors. (B05-239, B05-209, B04-131, B06-152, B05-199)
- **Explicit.** They range from a cubicle that barely fits three pupils to a bath the size of a swimming pool. (B02-072, B04-142)

## Conflicts

- Myrtle's bathroom is on the first floor by Hermione's word and on the second by narration and Book 4: see that place. (B02-045, B04-115)

## Moaning Myrtle’s bathroom

A neglected girls' toilet opening off the attack corridor, on the second floor by most evidence, though one character says the first. The door has a brass knob and an Out of Order sign. Inside, one big cracked mirror hangs over a row of stone sinks, the stone floor is damp, stubs of candle give dim light, and wooden cubicles with scratched doors stand in a line, at least four by inference, with Myrtle's at the end. Cubicles are small: three pupils fit in one only with difficulty. The sink in front of Myrtle's cubicle hides the way to the Chamber of Secrets: a copper tap marked with a tiny snake answers Parseltongue, and the sink drops away to expose a pipe big enough for a grown man. Other pupils avoid the room, which Myrtle's tantrums flood. Its floor is disputed: most journeys and both Book 4 statements support the second floor, but this is not settled.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** At least four cubicles stand in a line, with Myrtle's at the end. Because: Ron says she was three toilets from their brewing cubicle (B02-130), and hers is the end one (B02-059). Harry also uses a middle one (B02-089). (B02-059, B02-089, B02-130)
- **Explicit.** Three pupils fit into one cubicle only with difficulty, and transformed bodies would not fit together at all. (B02-072, B02-089)
- **Explicit.** The pipe exposed beneath that sink is big enough for an adult man to go down. (B02-140)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Book 4 places it on the second floor, three floors below the fifth-floor Prefects' bathroom. (B04-115, B04-145, B04-131)
- **Explicit.** Its door opens straight off the attack corridor, a few paces past Filch's chair. (B02-057)
- **Explicit.** It is about five minutes' walk from the library. (B02-062)
- **Explicit.** From the dungeons the way runs up to the Entrance Hall and on up the marble staircase. (B02-091, B02-095)
- **Reported.** Myrtle travels by pipes: she appears on the Prefects' bathroom taps and says a flush can carry her down to the lake. (B04-145)
- **Reported.** Myrtle says she died here fifty years earlier, in the end cubicle. (B02-139)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** One big mirror, cracked and spotted, hangs over a row of stone sinks with chipped edges. (B02-058)
- **Explicit.** The floor is stone and usually damp, lit dimly by low candle stubs in holders. (B02-058, B02-089)
- **Explicit.** Cubicles have wooden doors, flaking and scratched, and one door hangs off its hinges. (B02-058)
- **Explicit.** Cubicle doors have working locks with keyholes. (B02-072)
- **Inference.** The sinks face the cubicles. Because: Myrtle points to the sink in front of her own toilet, and it is that sink which hides the pipe (B02-139, B02-140). (B02-139, B02-140)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The door's sign says Out of Order, and other pupils stay away, which makes it private. (B02-057, B02-062)
- **Explicit.** One copper tap on the sink before Myrtle's cubicle bears a tiny engraved snake; spoken Parseltongue makes it glow and spin, and the sink sinks from view. (B02-140)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Myrtle's cubicle has a toilet with a cistern, on which she sits, and a bowl she dives into. (B02-059)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** The sink slid back into place by itself when four people flew up out of the pipe. (B02-156)
- **Reported.** It has been out of order because Myrtle's tantrums flood it. (B02-045)
- **Explicit.** In early 1993 a flood soaked walls and floor, put out the candles and spread under the door. (B02-098)
- **Explicit.** In late 1992 a cauldron sat on one cubicle's toilet over a portable waterproof fire. (B02-072)

### Conflicts

- First floor against second. Hermione says the first floor (B02-045). Narration takes Harry up the marble staircase to the first floor, up a further flight and round the second floor to the corridor outside this door (B02-046, B02-057). Book 4 says the second floor outright, and three floors below the fifth-floor bathroom (B04-115, B04-145, B04-131). (B02-045, B02-046, B02-057, B04-115, B04-145, B04-131)
- Lockhart's office supports the first floor. It lies on the second-floor corridor (B02-033), yet it is just upstairs from this corridor (B02-048), and the nearest stairs lead down from it to here (B02-138). (B02-033, B02-048, B02-138)
- McGonagall's first-floor study is reached moments after leaving the bathroom, with no stairs mentioned (B02-157, B01-103). This is a weak point for the first floor. (B02-157, B01-103)
- Against the hospital wing, Book 2 has it both ways: the ward is up from here (B02-095), yet this corridor is one floor up from the ward (B02-097). (B02-095, B02-097)
- Weight of the journeys, not a settled answer: the narrated climb, both Book 4 statements and the fifth-floor count all support the second floor. The first-floor reading rests on Hermione's remark and on Lockhart's office. (B02-046, B04-115, B04-145, B02-045)

### Open questions

- The total number of cubicles and sinks is never given.

## Prefects’ bathroom

The prefects' private bathroom on the fifth floor, four doors along to the left of the statue of Boris the Bewildered. It is a large room of white marble lit by a chandelier of candles, with windows hung in long white linen. A rectangular bath the size of a swimming pool lies sunken in the centre of the floor, deep enough that Harry can hardly stand in it, with a diving board and about a hundred golden taps around its edge, each with a jewel of a different colour in the handle. Taps pour hot water with bubbles, foam and scented clouds. One gold-framed painting of a sleeping mermaid hangs on the wall, and a big pile of towels sits in a corner. The door opens to a password and bolts from inside. Prefects and, from 1996, Quidditch captains may use it.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The sunken rectangular bath is the size of a swimming pool. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** About a hundred golden taps stand around its edge. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** Harry can hardly stand in the deep water, and he swims lengths. (B04-142)
- **Estimate.** The bath is at least about 15 m by 5 m, in a room of at least about 20 m by 10 m. Because: About 100 taps at an assumed 0.4 m each need 40 m of edge: 2 × (15 + 5) = 40. An assumed 2.5 m of floor all round gives 20 m by 10 m, or 200 m². (B04-142)

### Where it is

- **Reported.** It is on the fifth floor, four doors along to the left of the statue of Boris the Bewildered. (B04-131, B04-141)
- **Explicit.** Myrtle's toilets lie three floors below it. (B04-145)
- **Explicit.** The nearest staircase lies along the corridor in the direction away from Gryffindor Tower. (B04-147)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Walls, floor and bath are white marble, under a chandelier filled with candles. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** Windows are hung with long white linen curtains. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** A diving board stands at the bath. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** Footsteps and loud sounds echo off the marble. (B04-142)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The door opens to a password, Pine-fresh in January 1995, and bolts from inside. (B04-131, B04-141)
- **Reported.** Use is limited to prefects, and a Quidditch captain ranks with them. (B04-131, B06-007)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** No two tap handles share a jewel colour. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** Fluffy white towels are piled in a corner. (B04-142)
- **Explicit.** The only painting is a gold-framed blonde mermaid asleep on a rock, her hair moving as she snores. (B04-143)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Different taps give football-sized pink and blue bubbles, thick white foam, scented purple clouds or bouncing jets. (B04-144)
- **Explicit.** The bath fills very quickly for its size. (B04-144)
- **Explicit.** Myrtle appeared sitting on a tap and left up it. (B04-145)

## Sixth-floor boys’ bathroom

A tiled boys' bathroom on the sixth floor, one floor below the seventh-floor corridor, reached by a staircase and a passage. It has a sink facing a cracked mirror, toilet cubicles with cisterns, a wall lamp and a bin. A duel wrecked and flooded it in May 1997.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** A flat run from the Room of Requirement to this bathroom took Harry about a minute. (B06-152)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It is on the sixth floor, reached from the seventh by a marble staircase and a passage. Because: The Map shows it one floor below the seventh-floor corridor, and Harry takes the staircase down to it (B06-152). (B06-152)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The room is tiled, with a grimy sink facing a cracked mirror, cubicles, cisterns, a wall lamp and a bin. (B06-153)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** May 1997: spells shattered the lamp, blew up the bin and smashed a cistern, and water spread across the slippery floor. (B06-154)

### Open questions

- The text does not say whether this is the cracked-mirror boys' bathroom of B06-130, which Harry visits after Defence; that one is filed separately.

## Girls’ toilets of the troll attack

The girls' toilets where a twelve-foot troll was fought on Hallowe'en 1991: an upstairs room off a side passage, floor not stated. The door has a key in its outer lock. Inside are toilets and sinks fixed to the walls, with metal pipes. The room must clear a twelve-foot troll swinging a club. The text never links it to Myrtle's bathroom.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** A twelve-foot troll walked through its door and moved around inside, so door and ceiling must clear it unless it stooped. Because: The troll's height is narrated and it enters the room through the doorway (NEW-inner-4). (B01-148)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies upstairs from the Great Hall, off a passage reached by side corridors; no floor is stated. (B01-057, B01-059)
- **Inference.** It is probably below the third floor. Because: On the corridor before the toilets, Harry says Snape is heading for the third floor (B01-057), which suggests they are still below it. It is a remark, not a statement of floor. (B01-057)
- **Inference.** It lies at least two floors below the Fat Lady's portrait. Because: Harry and Ron climb two floors before they even start talking, and only then reach the portrait (B01-060). (B01-060)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The door has a key in its outer lock; inside are toilets and wall-fixed sinks with taps and metal pipes. (B01-058)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** 31 October 1991: the troll knocked sinks off the walls, and noise of the fight carried to floors below. (B01-059)

### Open questions

- Whether this is Myrtle's bathroom or another room: the text never says, and this room's door has a key where Myrtle's has a sign.

## Other bathrooms and toilets

Other washing places around the castle. This is a group of separate rooms, not one bathroom. Known instances: a boys' bathroom near the Room of Requirement on the seventh floor, a toilet on the fourth floor, several bathrooms on the second floor, a girls' bathroom one floor below Transfiguration, an old tiled boys' bathroom with a cracked mirror, and unlocated toilets. What they share is cubicles, sinks and mirrors, and that Myrtle can turn up in them. Their spread implies washing places on many floors.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** Bathrooms exist on at least the second, fourth, fifth, sixth and seventh floors. Because: Each floor has a named example: seventh (B05-199), sixth (B06-152), fifth (B04-131), fourth (B05-209), second (B05-239). (B05-199, B05-209, B05-239, B04-131, B06-152)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** A boys' bathroom stands along the seventh-floor corridor, to the right of the Room of Requirement. (B05-199)
- **Reported.** Montague is reported found jammed in a toilet on the fourth floor. (B05-209)
- **Explicit.** The second floor has more than one bathroom. (B05-239)
- **Explicit.** A girls' bathroom lies one floor below the Transfiguration classroom. (B06-082)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** An old tiled boys' bathroom, visited after Defence, has a cracked mirror and toilets in cubicles. (B06-130)
- **Reported.** Girls' toilets have several cubicles, all occupied at once in one report. (B05-181)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** In spring 1993 pupils could use bathrooms only with a teacher. (B02-112)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Neville's toad turned up in a corner of some unlocated toilets. (B01-144)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** May 1996: Peeves tore the taps from the second-floor bathrooms and flooded that floor. (B05-239)
- **Reported.** Myrtle says she can visit bathrooms other than her own, and is reported appearing in all of them. (B06-130)
