# Owlery and West Tower

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/owlery.md)

West Tower is the tower that carries the Owlery. It rises above the seventh floor, though it is not one of the three highest towers, and seventh-floor windows are counted from it. Climb its spiral stair and the steps beyond to a door with a handle, and you enter the Owlery at the top: a circular stone room with unglazed windows, straw and droppings underfoot, and hundreds upon hundreds of owls on perches and rafters rising to a vaulted ceiling. Windows look over the Forest and Hagrid's cabin. The tower's top also has battlements, reached by a doorway from a tightly spiralling stone stair.

## Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** Hundreds upon hundreds of owls roost on perches climbing all the way to the tower's top. (B04-035, B05-063)
- **Inference.** West Tower is lower than Gryffindor, Ravenclaw and Astronomy Towers. Because: A 1998 plan names those three as the highest, leaving it out. (B07-068)

## Where it is

- **Inference.** The Owlery is at the top of West Tower, which rises above the seventh floor. Because: The top is named outright. A Hippogriff carrying a passenger from a seventh-floor window has to climb to reach West Tower's top. (B03-155, B04-034)

## Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The Owlery is circular and of stone with unglazed windows. The top of the tower has battlements. (B03-155, B04-035)

## Getting there

- **Explicit.** A spiral stair and steps reach a hinged door. A tightly spiralling stone stair also links the battlements to a corridor. (B03-156, B04-192, B05-064)

## Conflicts

- A rising route from the seventh-floor portrait passes the fourth-floor corridor on the way to the Owlery. (B01-053, B04-034)
- Vaulted ceiling and rafters at the tower's top sit with walkable battlements at the same top. (B03-155, B05-063)

## Open questions

## Owlery

The Owlery is a circular stone room at the top of West Tower, home to the school owls and pupils' own owls. Its windows have no glass, so it is cold, windy and bright. Hundreds upon hundreds of owls of all breeds roost on long lines of perches and on rafters that climb all the way to the tower's top, under a vaulted ceiling. Straw, droppings and small skeletons cover the floor. It is reached by a spiral stair and steps up to a door with a handle. Its windows look over the Forest, Hagrid's cabin and the grounds, and a thrown owl fell twelve feet there before flying.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It houses hundreds upon hundreds of owls of all breeds. (B04-035, B05-063)
- **Explicit.** Perches in long lines climb all the way to the tower's top, with high rafters and a vaulted ceiling far enough up that Hedwig can sit out of reach. (B04-035, B04-065, B05-063)
- **Estimate.** Allow at least 10 m across and about two storeys high. Because: Assumes 0.3 m of perch per bird: 300 owls need 90 m of perch. A 10 m circle has 31 m of wall, so three rings of perches at the least, stacked upwards. (B04-035, B04-065)
- **Explicit.** An owl thrown from a window dropped twelve feet clear before recovering. That is the air below the sill, not the height above ground. (B04-098)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It stands at the top of West Tower. (B04-034)
- **Explicit.** Leaving it, the way comes to the entrance of a corridor running towards the castle's west wing. (B05-064)
- **Reported.** From the Room of Requirement, on the seventh floor, Harry judged it nearer than the dormitories. (B05-198)
- **Explicit.** Its windows overlook Forest treetops, Hagrid's cabin and the misty grounds, and in 1995 the Durmstrang ship's sails and the Beauxbatons carriage too. (B04-180, B04-192, B05-065)
- **Explicit.** Owls can be seen flying round the Owlery tower from the lawns near the Quidditch pitch. (B05-066)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** It is a circular stone room, cold and windy since its windows are unglazed holes in the wall. (B04-035, B05-063)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A spiral stair and steps climb to a hinged door with a handle. (B04-192, B05-064)
- **Explicit.** Routes from the seventh-floor portrait are described as going up to it. A longer way in 1995 passed many windows and a statue of Wilfred the Wistful. (B04-034, B05-061, B05-062)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** The floor is covered with straw, owl droppings and the bones of mice and voles coughed up by the birds. It is slippery and crunches underfoot. (B04-035, B05-063)
- **Explicit.** Pupils' owls roost and feed here with the school owls, which anyone can borrow. About half the perches were empty at one dawn as owls came back from hunting. (B01-033, B04-033, B04-065, B04-192)

### Conflicts

- Book 4 sends Harry up from the seventh-floor portrait yet past a vase on the fourth-floor corridor. Either he first went down to reach the West Tower, or the text is inconsistent. (B01-053, B04-034)
- The Owlery has rafters, a vaulted ceiling and perches up to the tower's top, yet the same tower top has walkable battlements. A vault with a walkway round or above it could fit both. (B03-155, B04-035, B05-063)

### Open questions

- Which floor the spiral stair starts from. That also decides whether the fourth-floor route was a real detour.

## West Tower

West Tower carries the Owlery at its top and battlements that took a landing Hippogriff with three riders. A doorway at the top leads down a tightly spiralling stone stair to a corridor. Seen from the lake shore it is a landmark for counting windows: Professor Flitwick's seventh-floor office is window thirteen counting to its right. Its top stands above the seventh floor, but it is not one of the three highest towers, so it must be lower than Gryffindor Tower.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** The office window is at least about 36 m along the wall from the tower. Because: Twelve window spaces at an assumed minimum of 3 m each: 12 x 3 = 36 m. The text gives no spacing. (B03-139, B03-152)
- **Inference.** Its top stands above the seventh floor. Because: A Hippogriff flies upwards from the seventh-floor office window to reach the battlements. (B03-139, B03-155)
- **Inference.** It is lower than Gryffindor, Ravenclaw and Astronomy Towers. Because: A 1998 plan names those three as the highest, leaving West Tower out. (B07-068)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The Owlery is at the top of West Tower. (B04-034)
- **Reported.** Flitwick's seventh-floor office is window thirteen counting to the right from West Tower, and those windows can be counted from the lake shore. (B03-139, B03-152)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The top has battlements that took a landing Hippogriff, with three people on it. (B03-155)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A doorway at the top opens on a tightly spiralling stone stair down to a corridor. (B03-156)
- **Explicit.** From that corridor the way to the hospital wing ran down one staircase, then another. Stair counts here do not give a floor count. (B03-156)

### Open questions

- How the battlements sit relative to the Owlery's vault, and which floor the corridor at the stair's foot is on.
