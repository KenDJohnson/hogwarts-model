# Greenhouses, gardens and lawns

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/greenhouses.md)

From the foot of the front steps the forecourt lawn opens out, large enough for the whole school to line up. At Christmas 1994 part of it became the conjured rose garden of the Yule Ball. Sloping lawns then fall away towards the lake, the stadium and Hagrid's cabin, with the flying lawn on the far side of the grounds from the Forest. Turning aside from the direct line to Hagrid's, a walker reaches the vegetable patch, with its low stone wall, and beyond it the greenhouses, behind the castle. There are at least three, numbered, and Greenhouse Three alone holds a class of about twenty with trays, a central bench and hanging flowers the size of umbrellas. A compost heap lies near them, and the Whomping Willow is in view in the distance. Going on from there leads round the Willow to the Forest.

## Greenhouses

The greenhouses are the school's glass teaching houses for Herbology, in the grounds behind the castle, beyond the vegetable patch. There are several, numbered: Greenhouse One for the first-years and Greenhouse Three for more dangerous plants. A Two is implied, the total is never given, and in mist pupils take longer to find the right one. Greenhouse Three holds a two-house class of about twenty at a central trestle bench, with deep trays of about a hundred seedlings, stored pots and compost, and flowers as big as umbrellas hanging from the ceiling, so these are large working halls and not garden sheds. They have glass walls and roofs, lockable doors and soil to grow in. From outside them the Whomping Willow can be seen in the distance, and pupils leaving them come within calling range of the castle's stone steps. A compost heap lies near by, and people can hide behind them.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** There are at least three: Greenhouse One and Greenhouse Three are named, which implies a Two. Because: Numbers one and three are used as names. No other number appears anywhere in the books. (B02-019)
- **Inference.** The total is not given. They are enough alike and numerous enough that mist slows pupils in finding the right one. Because: Mist delays the class in locating the right building. (B06-065, B01-029)
- **Explicit.** Greenhouse Three takes a two-house class of about twenty, working four to a tray. (B02-020)
- **Inference.** Twenty pupils at four to a tray need at least five deep trays, together holding about a hundred Mandrake seedlings. Because: Twenty divided by four, with the hundred or so seedlings shown in a row of trays. (B02-020)
- **Estimate.** Allow Greenhouse Three at least about 15 m by 8 m. Because: Twenty pupils at trays need about 3 square metres each with aisles, so 60, plus a central bench, stored pots and sacks, and dangerous plants kept out of reach. An assumption, to stop it reading as a garden shed. (B02-020, B02-019)
- **Explicit.** Flowers as big as umbrellas hang from the ceiling of Three, and cabbage-sized blooms open in late spring. (B02-019, B02-116)

### Where it is

## School vegetable patch

The vegetable patch is the school's cultivated ground between the castle and the greenhouses, crossed on the usual walk to Herbology. The books also call it vegetable patches, vegetable gardens and a vegetable path, so it may be several beds with walkways. It is reached from the castle or from Hagrid's lesson ground. It lies a little off the direct line from the front steps to Hagrid's cabin, and a low stone wall stands there. From it the dark mass of the Forest and the far-off cabin lights can be seen. School cabbages suffer from slugs, and the ground floods in heavy rain. Its size is not given.

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies between the castle and the greenhouses, so pupils cross it on the way to Herbology. (B02-018, B02-118, B04-023, B05-003, B06-065)
- **Explicit.** It is also crossed on the way from Hagrid's lesson ground to the greenhouses. (B05-053)
- **Explicit.** A swerving car crossed greenhouses, then patch, then lawns, then reached the Willow. (B02-006)
- **Explicit.** It is a detour from the front steps to Hagrid's, not on the direct line. (B06-137)
- **Explicit.** From it the Forest and the far-off lights of Hagrid's cabin are in view. (B06-137)

### Shape and structure

- **Inference.** It is probably several beds with walkways, as the text calls it patches, gardens and a path. Because: The same ground is named as vegetable patches, vegetable gardens and a vegetable path in different books. (B05-003, B03-142, B04-023)
- **Explicit.** A low stone wall stands at it. (B06-137)

### What is inside

- **Reported.** Hagrid says slugs are ruining the school cabbages; the text does not say this bed grows them. (B02-002)
- **Explicit.** After a talk with Sprout here in spring 1997, Slughorn carries off an armful of leafy plants. (B06-137)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** It floods in storms: sodden in September 1994, under water in October 1995. (B04-023, B05-107)

### Open questions

- Size and outline. The text says only that it is walked across.

## Flying-lesson lawn

The flying lawn is a smooth stretch of grass used for first-year flying lessons, on the far side of the grounds from the Forbidden Forest, reached down the sloping lawns from the front steps. An oak stands close by.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** A lesson sets out twenty school brooms in lines for two houses of first-years. (B01-041)
- **Reported.** The oak is tall: a hovering pupil is level with its top branches, and McGonagall speaks of a fifty-foot dive here. (B01-041, B01-157)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies across the grounds from the Forest, which shows in the distance, reached down the sloping lawns from the front steps. (B01-040)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The way back to the classrooms is up the front steps and the marble staircase. (B01-042)

## Front lawns and slopes

Front lawns and slopes is a group heading for the open grass that falls away from the castle front towards the lake, the Forest edge and the pitch; it is not one lawn. Known stretches are the forecourt lawn before the front steps, where the whole school can line up; the slope down to Hagrid's cabin; the slope to the Quidditch stadium; the slope to the lake; and the ground around the Whomping Willow. They share open grass and a steady fall away from the castle, so every return is uphill. Size comes from what they take: the school in rows with a house-sized carriage and a dozen elephant-sized horses landing beside it, and open ground where Death Eaters drew up opposite the doors in 1998. No stretch is measured from the castle.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The forecourt takes the whole school in rows, with a house-sized carriage and twelve elephant-sized winged horses landing beside it. (B04-041, B04-043)
- **Explicit.** The Beauxbatons carriage later stood on this open ground 200 yards from Hagrid's front door, with a paddock for the horses alongside. (B04-052)
- **Explicit.** A lesson table stood about 10 yards from Hagrid's door, with a class spread out on the grass. (B05-051)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Slope to Hagrid's: it runs from the front doors down to the cabin at the Forest edge, with a five-minute walk along the trees to a paddock. (B03-020, B05-051, B04-024, B04-077, B07-118)
- **Explicit.** Slope to the stadium: it leads down from the steps to the stadium and changing rooms, and a stretch of lawn links the stands to Hagrid's. (B05-066, B05-122, B04-185, B05-242, B05-103)

## Yule Ball gardens

The Yule Ball gardens were a temporary rose garden and grotto conjured on the lawn directly in front of the castle for Christmas night 1994. Curving decorative paths ran among rose bushes, big stone figures and carved benches, lit by hundreds of real fairies, and led to a tall fountain with a bench facing it.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** Hundreds of real fairies served as lights in the bushes. (B04-123)
- **Inference.** It was large enough to hide several groups from one another along winding paths with corners. Because: Couples hide in bushes, two teachers come round a corner unseen, and people on a bench do not notice listeners behind a stone reindeer. (B04-129, B04-130)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It filled an area of lawn directly before the castle, visible through the open front doors and reached down the front steps. (B04-123, B04-129)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Curving decorative paths ran among rose bushes, big stone figures of Father Christmas and reindeer, and carved benches. (B04-123, B04-129)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** A tall fountain stood beyond a large stone reindeer, with a stone bench facing the water. (B04-129)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Christmas 1994 only: the bushes were conjured, and Snape blasted some apart, while Madame Maxime sent swarms of fairies up as she pushed through. (B04-123, B04-130)

### Open questions

- Whether the fountain was conjured with the rest or stands there permanently. The text does not say.
