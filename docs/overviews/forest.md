# Forbidden Forest and its inhabitants' spaces

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/forest.md)

The Forest area runs from the lawn’s edge inward. At the edge, near Hagrid’s cabin, a narrow earth track enters thick trees and forks a little way in. Along the outside, about five minutes round the trees, is the 1993 creature paddock, and far beyond the Beauxbatons paddock, out of sight of castle and lake, the 1994 dragon enclosure. Inside, about ten minutes’ walk brings a class to the dark, snow-free Thestral feeding place. About twenty minutes along the spider path the sky disappears; leaving the path, the hollow of the Acromantula colony lies at least another half-hour on, downhill, towards the heart of the Forest, ten rough minutes by car from the edge. Centaurs claim the whole wood; fifty were seen at once in a dim clearing far along that same old path. A long way past the Thestrals and off the path, through brambles and thorn, then fifteen more minutes past nettles, is the clearing where Grawp was tied in 1996. The dead unicorn of 1992 lay nearly half an hour beyond the regrouping point. Treat every walking time as real but no bearing as known: the books give no map, and the relative positions of these sites must not be invented.

## Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** Treat the deep sites as at least about 2 km inside a Forest at least 4 km across. Because: Timed walking gives about 2 km at 4 km/h on path and 1.5 km/h off it, and the car’s ten minutes agrees. The hollow lies towards the heart. (B02-122, B02-124, B02-128)

## Where it is

- **Inference.** The Thestral place is shallow compared with the rest. Because: Hagrid says that visit did not go in so far, and the Grawp walk runs a long way past it. (B05-243, B05-244)
- **Inference.** The sites overlap ground the centaurs hold: the spider path passes one of their clearings, and the way back from Grawp’s clearing crosses their patrol. Because: Hermione deliberately leads Umbridge along the old path to meet them, and Magorian’s group stops Hagrid in knotgrass on the return. (B05-279, B05-280, B05-249)

## Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Routes thicken going in and thin coming out; on the spider path the sky was lost after about twenty minutes, and it returns shortly before the edge. (B02-122, B05-244, B02-128, B05-250)

## Getting there

- **Explicit.** Round the outside from the cabin: about five minutes to the 1993 creature paddock; much further, past the horse paddock, to the dragon enclosure. (B03-020, B04-078)
- **Explicit.** Inward from the edge: about ten minutes to the Thestral place, about twenty along the spider path before the sky disappears. (B05-139, B02-122)
- **Explicit.** Deep sites: half an hour or more off the path to the hollow; fifteen minutes past a far off-path halt to Grawp; nearly half an hour past the regrouping point to the unicorn. (B02-122, B05-245, B01-112)
- **Explicit.** Coming out: ten rough minutes by car from the hollow, and ten on foot from where the Grawp route rejoins a path. (B02-128, B05-250)

## Open questions

- No bearing, map or route junction between any two sites is given; keep the times and sequence only.

## Forbidden Forest

The Forbidden Forest is the great old wood on the school grounds, forbidden to pupils. Its near edge lies at Hagrid’s cabin, at the foot of the lawns, and from the front doors its treetops stand in the distance, towards the June sunset. No size is given, but times on foot show it is very large: twenty minutes on a path takes you out of sight of the sky, and the deepest known sites are an hour or more in. Near the edge a class can walk among the trees; further in they crowd together, branches tangle, the canopy shuts out all light, and paths fade into roots, brambles, thorn and nettles. Narrow earth tracks run inward and fork, and at least two separate routes exist. Clearings large and small open within it. Oak, beech, yew, sycamore and pine are named. The only height given is twenty feet, for pines a giant can uproot, and others tower well above that. Centaurs, unicorns, Thestrals, giant spiders and Bowtruckles live here. The text supplies no map.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** Treat the Forest as at least 4 km across and do not model it as a strip of woodland behind the cabin. Because: Twenty minutes on a path at 4 km/h is 1.3 km and half an hour off it at 1.5 km/h is 0.75 km, about 2 km before the last carried stretch. Ten minutes by car at 15 km/h agrees. If that is near the middle, the wood is at least 4 km wide. (B02-122, B02-124, B02-128)
- **Inference.** Deep sites lie an hour or so from the edge: twenty minutes on a path and half an hour off it still leave the spider hollow further on. Because: Those are the timed legs of the 1993 walk; the unicorn and Grawp routes also add half an hour or more beyond untimed earlier stretches. (B02-122, B02-124, B01-112, B05-245)
- **Reported.** The only stated tree height is Hermione’s twenty feet, for the pines Grawp rips up. (B05-253)
- **Inference.** Many trees stand well above that: Harry lands a broom in a towering beech, an oak towers too, and Grawp’s head fits just under the boughs at a clearing edge. Because: Towering is the narrator’s word for beech and oak. The sixteen-foot giant’s head gives a floor for the lowest branches, not the treetops. (B01-088, B01-109, B05-282)
- **Estimate.** Model mature oak and beech at no less than about 20 m, and treat 6 m pines as the small end of the wood. Because: Twenty feet is about 6 m. Real trees of these species reach 25 to 30 m, and a flyer lowers himself through the top branches here; the text gives no figure. (B05-253, B01-088)
- **Inference.** Gaps still pass large bodies on some routes: spiders with heads ten feet up carry boys between the trees, and a car winds out by the widest gaps, losing a mirror to an oak. Because: Off-path movement is narrated, not surveyed. A car needs gaps of about 2 m on that one route, so spacing is tight but not solid. (B02-124, B02-128)
- **Explicit.** Single trees are massive: one oak takes Hagrid and two pupils behind it. (B01-109)

### Where it is

- **Reported.** Dumbledore places it in the school grounds; Hagrid’s cabin at the foot of the lawns marks its near edge. (B01-019, B04-018, B05-021, B01-037)
- **Inference.** From the front steps and lawns it stands towards the setting sun in spring and summer, so broadly west of the castle front. Because: The sun sinks behind or towards its treetops on three evenings, seen from the steps and from Gryffindor Tower. One morning passage disagrees; see conflicts. (B03-113, B05-278, B06-135)
- **Explicit.** Its treetops look distant from the front doors; the flying lawn lies across the grounds from it, and it stands to the right on the way down to the pitch. (B05-251, B01-040, B05-066)
- **Inference.** Part of its margin faces the lake. Because: From the funeral seats on the shore Harry looks out over the water towards the Forest and sees centaurs among its trees. (B06-209)
- **Reported.** A school boundary lies on this side of the grounds: Hagrid says Grawp smashed through it by the Forest in 1998. (B07-072)
- **Explicit.** A gale in its trees is heard in Gryffindor Tower, birds bursting from it are seen from the front doors, and walkers entering it are visible from Umbridge’s office window. (B03-049, B05-251, B05-284)
- **Explicit.** From the Owlery one looks down on its canopy beyond Hagrid’s cabin, swaying in any breeze. (B04-180, B05-065)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Density rises inward: from the air the canopy hides a running man, after twenty minutes no stars show, and further in no light reaches the ground. (B01-088, B02-122, B05-279, B05-244, B01-114)
- **Explicit.** The trees are old, close-set and gnarled, with tangled branches; Harry contrasts a younger, wider-spaced wood with clearer ground. (B07-120, B07-016, B01-112)
- **Explicit.** A narrow winding earth track leaves the edge and forks left and right a little way in; deeper in, paths become overgrown and nearly impossible to trace. (B01-108, B01-112, B05-244, B01-111, B05-250)
- **Inference.** At least two inward routes exist: Hagrid’s way towards Grawp and the older path Harry followed towards Aragog. Because: Harry notes that Hermione has taken a different path from the Grawp visit. Any junction between them is unstated. (B05-279, B05-244)
- **Explicit.** The floor is leaf-covered earth with roots and stumps; brambles, thorn, nettles and knotgrass fill the off-path ground, which falls towards the hollow. Running water is heard near one path. (B01-108, B01-109, B02-122, B05-244, B05-245, B05-249)
- **Explicit.** Smaller clearings occur throughout, such as a shadowy one below a beech (1992) and a ring of thick trees under a branch roof with room for a car (1993). (B01-088, B02-123)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** Pupils are barred by a rule repeated year after year, not by any barrier; Hagrid, detentions, lessons and work parties all go in. (B01-019, B04-018, B05-021, B01-107, B05-139, B07-020)

### What is inside

- **Reported.** Inhabitants include centaurs, unicorns, Thestrals, giant spiders, Bowtruckles, birds and deer; Hippogriffs come out of it in 1993 and a car ran wild in it. Werewolves are only rumoured. (B01-019, B01-115, B03-021, B04-001, B05-141, B05-248, B05-283, B06-005, B07-006, B07-020, B05-065)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Giants damage it: Grawp uproots and smashes trees from 1996, and two others knock trees down on the march out in May 1998. (B05-248, B05-282, B07-124)
- **Reported.** Sirius Black says he lived here as a dog during 1993–94; no den or shelter is described. (B03-132)
- **Explicit.** In May 1998 it creaks on a windless night, and Harry drops the Resurrection Stone somewhere in it, never recovered. (B07-095, B07-121, B07-135)

### Conflicts

- Evening passages put the setting sun behind the Forest as seen from the steps and Gryffindor Tower, yet one clear morning Harry squints into the sun while watching its edge from a Gryffindor dormitory. (B03-113, B05-278, B06-135, B05-227)

### Open questions

- Size and outline are never given. Keep the walking times, choose a shape no smaller than the estimated extent, and do not fix bearings between sites.
- Whether the wood continues past the school boundary is unstated.

## Forest edge

The Forest edge is the line where the lawns meet the trees, running past Hagrid’s cabin and on around the grounds. Its outermost ground is earthy and scattered with twigs, with single landmark trees: a large sycamore near the cabin, wide and tall oaks, and clumps that screen whatever lies behind them. Coming out of the Forest the trees thin gradually, then stop at open grass. It is long: about five minutes’ walk separates the cabin from the 1993 creature paddock, and further round it goes so far that castle and lake vanish. Gryffindor Tower overlooks it, and from the edge near the cabin one can see the castle’s front doors and the Whomping Willow; elsewhere the stadium is in view. A few minutes’ run uphill leads back to the castle.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The screen closes fast: a short way inside the trees Harry cannot see Hagrid’s garden at all, though he can still hear the back door and voices. (B03-145)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The edge lies across the grounds from the castle, with Hagrid’s cabin standing on it. (B01-037, B01-096, B01-107, B07-118)
- **Inference.** It runs a long way: five minutes’ walk from the cabin to the creature paddock, and far enough round beyond the horse paddock to lose sight of castle and lake. Because: Both walks follow the tree line. The first is timed; the second is judged from Harry’s impatience and his hurried return. (B03-020, B04-078, B04-091)
- **Explicit.** From trees near the cabin one sees its front door and, with a small head movement, the distant castle doors; a clump further round commands the Whomping Willow. (B03-143, B03-147, B03-149)
- **Explicit.** A route from the greenhouses past the Whomping Willow reaches the trees while staying hidden from Hagrid’s front door. (B03-142)
- **Explicit.** From the trees at the end of one Forest path the Quidditch stadium is in view, with sloping lawns leading up from it to the castle. (B05-250)
- **Explicit.** Gryffindor Tower overlooks it: dormitory and common-room windows show figures moving along the edge, teaching there or leaving the trees. (B03-104, B05-227, B05-259)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Named edge trees are a large sycamore near the cabin, a wide oak that hides two people, a tall oak beyond the horse paddock, and screening clumps. (B02-121, B03-143, B04-187, B03-147, B04-078, B04-097)
- **Explicit.** The outer ground is earthy and strewn with twigs, with roots among the first trees; it was dry under one clump in June 1994. (B07-119, B05-052, B04-190, B03-147)
- **Explicit.** Coming out of the Forest the trees thin gradually until sky shows, and then open ground begins. (B02-128, B05-250, B07-124)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A narrow earth track starts at the very edge not far from the cabin; elsewhere people simply step in among the trees or come out of them near his door. (B01-108, B02-121, B05-139, B05-243, B02-129, B05-137)
- **Inference.** From the trees just past the horse paddock the front doors are only minutes away uphill at a run. Because: Harry sprints up through the grounds and into the castle, and is in a second-floor corridor about five minutes later. (B04-187, B04-188)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** In May 1998 Dementors patrol the outer trees, and Voldemort halts here to address the castle before marching up the lawn. (B07-119, B07-124, B07-125)
- **Explicit.** The open margin takes temporary set-ups: a champions’ tent before the screening trees (1994), a tethered unicorn (1995), and the house-sized carriage landing here (1997). (B04-091, B04-133, B06-202)

### Open questions

- The order of features along the edge is only partly fixed: cabin, then carriage and horse paddock, then the dragon site. Where the 1993 paddock, the Willow clump and the stadium view fall in that line is unknown.

## Aragog’s hollow and spider colony

Aragog’s hollow is a vast, tree-free depression deep in the Forest, towards its heart, where the giant spider colony lived. About twenty minutes on a path, then at least half an hour off it going downhill, still leaves you short of it; the car escape to the Forest edge took ten minutes. A steep slope falls to a leaf-covered floor open to the stars, with a dome of misty webbing at the centre. Aragog was as big as a small elephant, his descendants as big as carthorses, and they packed the hollow in 1993. Voldemort camped here in May 1998, with the spiders driven out.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** Place it at least about 2 km inside the Forest. Because: Twenty minutes at 4 km/h plus half an hour at 1.5 km/h is about 2 km before the carried stretch; ten minutes by car at 15 km/h is 2.5 km. (B02-122, B02-128)
- **Explicit.** A vast hollow cleared of trees and open to the stars, with a leaf-strewn floor and a steep rim a car can drive down and climb. (B02-124, B02-125, B02-128)
- **Estimate.** Make the hollow at least 100 m across at the rim. Because: Fifty carthorse-sized spiders needing 10 m by 10 m each take 5,000 m², a circle about 80 m wide, before adding the web and a slope for a car. (B02-124, B02-125)
- **Explicit.** Colony spiders are black, hairy and eight-eyed, as big as carthorses in 1993 and compared with a small car in 1998. (B02-124, B02-125, B07-089)
- **Inference.** A full-grown spider’s head can reach about ten feet above the ground. Because: Ron stares at a point that high just behind Harry at the moment one seizes him. This is one observation, not a rule for all of them. (B02-124)
- **Explicit.** Aragog is as big as a small elephant, grey-flecked and blind; he hatched at about Pekinese size. Hagrid got his dead body out of the Forest and heaved it into a pit. (B02-125, B06-144, B06-138, B06-140)
- **Inference.** No count is given, but the colony must number dozens at the very least. Because: From one pair it spread across the hollow, closed on the captives from all sides as a solid wall, and several were knocked aside by the car. (B02-125, B02-126, B02-128)
- **Estimate.** Allow the web dome at least 5 m of height and 10 m of width. Because: It shelters a spider as big as a small elephant, about 3 m high, with legs spreading several metres wider. (B02-125)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies deep in the Forest; the captives are carried towards its very heart. (B02-124, B06-049)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A dome of misty webbing stands at the centre, and Aragog comes out of it and backs into it. (B02-125)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** On foot: about twenty minutes along a path until no stars show, then what seems at least half an hour off it, slowly and downhill, then an untimed carried stretch. (B02-122, B02-123, B02-124)
- **Explicit.** The car takes about ten rough minutes from the hollow to the Forest edge, by the widest gaps. (B02-128)
- **Explicit.** Harry recognises the 1993 route in June 1996, by then obstructed by brambles, undergrowth and a fallen sapling. (B05-279)
- **Reported.** Hagrid alone could approach safely: he calls it unsafe for others in 1996, and after Aragog’s death in 1997 the colony no longer lets him near its webs. (B06-049, B06-138)
- **Explicit.** In May 1998 Harry reaches it minutes after meeting a patrol in deep trees; the march back runs through close-growing trees that gradually thin. (B07-120, B07-122, B07-124)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** In May 1998 Death Eaters camp here round a central fire, with two giants at the margin, Hagrid tied to a nearby tree and only remnants of the web. (B07-122, B07-123)
- **Explicit.** By then the spiders have been driven out, and some climb the castle walls during the battle. (B07-122, B07-089)

### Conflicts

- In 1993 it is a steep-sided hollow; in 1998 the same site is called a clearing, with no slope mentioned. (B02-124, B07-122)

### Open questions

- Depth of the hollow and its exact rim width are unstated.

## Centaur territory

The centaurs’ territory has no fixed boundary or settlement: the herd treats the whole Forest as its own, and what the books show are clearings where they are met. A dim clearing far along the old spider path held about fifty in June 1996. Smaller groups appear elsewhere, and in 1997 they watched a funeral from the trees towards the lake.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** About fifty armed centaurs surround three humans in one clearing in June 1996; Hagrid earlier says only that there are a fair few. (B05-280, B01-110)
- **Estimate.** Make that clearing at least 30 m across. Because: Fifty horse-bodied centaurs in one ring at 1.5 m spacing make a circle about 75 m round and 24 m wide, with the centre clear and room to move behind them. (B05-280)
- **Explicit.** Elsewhere they appear in small groups: Ronan and Bane in 1992, Magorian with four or five others in 1996. (B01-110, B05-249)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The 1996 clearing is dim and damp, on the old path towards Aragog after a long walk, past where the canopy shuts out light. Harry thinks it the middle of the Forest. (B05-279, B05-280, B05-318)
- **Explicit.** Other meetings happen in a clearing beyond the left fork (1992) and in thick knotgrass on the way back from Grawp’s clearing (1996). (B01-108, B01-110, B05-249)
- **Explicit.** In June 1997 they stand half-hidden among trees across the water from the funeral seats; their arrows fall far short of the crowd. (B06-209)

### Getting there

- **Reported.** They bar Hagrid from 1996 after his rescue of Firenze, and Firenze, banished, says that even going too near could start a fight. (B05-249, B05-195)

### What is inside

- **Inference.** No dwelling, camp or settlement is ever described; treat the clearings as meeting grounds, not a village. Because: Every sighting is of centaurs among trees or in clearings, and nothing built is mentioned. (B06-005, B06-209, B05-280)
- **Inference.** Classroom eleven, made to imitate Firenze’s natural habitat, suggests mossy earth, leafy trees, dappled green light, boulders and stumps. Because: Firenze says the room copies his home in the Forest. It is an enchanted classroom, not the place itself, so it shows character rather than layout. (B05-192)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** In June 1996 Grawp breaks into the clearing between two trunks, scatters the herd and smashes more trees chasing it. (B05-282)
- **Reported.** In May 1998 centaurs are present near the thinning trees as Voldemort’s march leaves the Forest. (B07-124)

### Conflicts

- Ownership is disputed: centaurs call it their Forest, Umbridge says the Ministry permits them certain land, and Hagrid denies they control who comes and goes. (B05-281, B05-249)

### Open questions

- Where the herd actually lives, and the limits of the ground it claims, are never shown.

## Unicorn clearing

The unicorn clearing is a deep-Forest clearing where a dead unicorn was found in 1992, nearly half an hour’s walk past the point where the two detention parties regrouped, with the path almost lost among thick trees. It is seen through the boughs of an ancient oak, with a bush at its edge.

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** It is seen through the tangled boughs of an ancient oak; a bush grows at its edge, and dark leaves cover the ground. (B01-112, B01-113)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** Nearly half an hour’s walk beyond the regrouping point, itself reached by an untimed stretch, until the path is almost lost among thick trees. (B01-112)
- **Explicit.** The way back to the path runs through a dense patch of trees, with low branches that a centaur’s rider must duck. (B01-114)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** In 1992 a dead white unicorn lay here, and a hooded figure fed on it until a centaur drove the figure off. (B01-113)

## Thestral clearing

This is the place about ten minutes’ walk into the Forest where Hagrid feeds the Hogwarts Thestrals, a dark stand of close-set trees that keeps out the snow, with gnarled yews among them. The herd’s real home is the Forest at large: it began with six animals and, if each of the hundred or so school coaches takes one, must now number about a hundred.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** A double class fits among the trunks, with half a cow laid on the ground and animals coming in between the trees. (B05-139, B05-140)
- **Reported.** Hagrid says the herd grew from one male and five females, and that Tenebrus was the first foal born in the Forest. (B05-141)
- **Inference.** If each of the hundred or so school coaches has its own animal, the herd numbers about a hundred. Because: About a hundred coaches wait at the start of term, each with a creature between its shafts, and Hagrid says pulling them is the herd’s main work. No census exists. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-008, B05-009, B05-141)
- **Explicit.** At least four come to one feed; eight or nine gather around two pupils deep in the Forest in June 1996. (B05-140, B05-285)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** They range well beyond this spot: one rose above the treetops in view of the Owlery in 1995, and others came to pupils near the centaur clearing, on a different path. (B05-065, B05-279, B05-285)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The trees stand so close that it is as dark as twilight and no snow lies on the ground; gnarled yews are named. (B05-139, B05-140)
- **Explicit.** They are large enough to ride with no saddle: in June 1996 Harry mounts from a stump, and they climb out steeply through the topmost branches. (B05-286, B05-287)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** About ten minutes’ walk in from the Forest edge with a class in winter, and not far in by Hagrid’s own account. (B05-139, B05-243)
- **Explicit.** The route to Grawp’s clearing passes this site and carries on a long way past it. (B05-244)

### What is inside

- **Reported.** Only people who have seen death can see the animals; in 1995 Harry, Neville and one Slytherin could. Hagrid has trained them to leave owls alone. (B05-142, B05-092)

### Conflicts

- The feeding place is a close stand of trees in the lesson chapter but is called a clearing when passed in May 1996. (B05-139, B05-244)

### Open questions

- Where the site lies relative to the track from the edge is unstated.

## Grawp’s clearing

Grawp’s clearing is the bare patch deep in the Forest where Hagrid kept his sixteen-foot half-brother tied up in 1996. It lies a long way past the Thestral site, off the path, through brambles and thorn and then fifteen more minutes past nettles. The giant had uprooted the trees around him, leaving bare ground ringed by heaped trunks and boughs, with ropes running from his wrists and ankles to the biggest surviving trees. He broke free in June 1996 and had moved to a mountain cave by that September.

### Size and numbers

- **Inference.** The walk in takes at least 25 minutes and probably well over 40. Because: Ten minutes to the Thestral site and the final fifteen are the only timed parts. The long stretch past the Thestrals and the slow off-path struggle come on top. (B05-139, B05-244, B05-245)
- **Estimate.** Place it at least about 1.5 km inside the Forest. Because: Ten minutes at 4 km/h plus fifteen at 2 km/h give 1.2 km, before adding the untimed stretches. (B05-139, B05-244, B05-245)
- **Estimate.** Make the bare ground at least 15 m across. Because: A 5 m giant lies curled near the middle, Hagrid stands 3 m from him with a long bough and backs away from him, and the ring of heaped trunks lies beyond that. (B05-246, B05-247)
- **Explicit.** Grawp is at least sixteen feet tall yet called small for a giant, with feet like sledges nearly Harry’s length and hands the size of beach umbrellas. (B05-247, B05-253, B05-282, B07-027)
- **Explicit.** Asleep, his curled back looks like an earth mound nearly Hagrid’s height and broad enough for three to stand on, with a boulder-sized head beside it. (B05-246)
- **Explicit.** Inside the ring Hagrid can stand about ten feet from him. (B05-246)
- **Explicit.** A towering pine with a nest in its upper branches stands within his reach; Hermione later puts such pines at twenty feet. (B05-248, B05-253)

### Where it is

- **Reported.** No other creature is met near it, which Harry puts down to the giant. Hagrid says Grawp feeds himself on birds and deer. (B05-319, B05-244, B05-248)
- **Reported.** From the castle front doors Harry thinks he sees birds burst up over distant treetops soon after, as though another tree had been pulled up. (B05-251)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A bare patch of earth with trees torn up at the roots all round; the trunks and boughs lie heaped into a rough fence. (B05-245, B05-246)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** From the cabin: a path past the Thestral site and a long way beyond, then off it towards the Forest’s heart through brambles and thorn that hold pupils up for minutes at a time. (B05-243, B05-244)
- **Explicit.** After a halt, fifteen more minutes past nettles bring the party to the barricade. (B05-245)
- **Explicit.** The way out skirts the same nettles to rejoin the path, then runs ten minutes to thinning trees with the stadium in view. (B05-250)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Ropes as thick as saplings run from his wrists and ankles to the largest nearby trunks, which creak when he stands. (B05-247)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** He bends that pine until the roots crack and has brought it down by the time the visitors are walking away. (B05-248)
- **Explicit.** By June 1996 he had snapped his ankle ropes and left. Hagrid later reports him calmer, and by September 1996 he has a mountain cave instead. (B05-282, B05-305, B06-023)

### Conflicts

- Going in, they pass a site ten minutes from the edge and continue a long way before leaving the path; coming out, they rejoin a path only ten minutes from thinning trees, near the stadium. (B05-139, B05-244, B05-250)

### Open questions

- Its bearing and its position relative to Aragog’s hollow are unstated; both lie towards the dark centre, on different paths.

## Dragon enclosure

The dragon enclosure was a temporary fenced compound for the first Triwizard task, set up in November 1994 well round the Forest edge, behind a clump of trees and out of sight of castle and lake. Thick plank fencing held four full-grown dragons that reared to fifty feet and lay like small hills when felled, with at least thirty keepers. On task day conjured stands with hundreds of spectators surrounded the same spot. It is a dated set-up, not a permanent site.

### Size and numbers

- **Estimate.** Place it at least 1 km from the castle front doors by the walking route. Because: Ten hurried minutes at 6 km/h is 1 km, and the site must also be beyond view of castle and lake. (B04-078)
- **Explicit.** Four full-grown dragons rear inside, their heads reaching fifty feet up on stretched necks; felled, each lies like a small hill and weighs several tons. (B04-079)
- **Reported.** A keeper gives their fire a twenty-foot range and says he has seen the Horntail reach forty feet. (B04-081)
- **Explicit.** At least thirty keepers work here, seven or eight to a dragon, and five of them carry the eggs in a blanket. (B04-079, B04-080)
- **Estimate.** Make the fenced area at least 70 m across. Because: Four animals each 15 to 20 m long, kept beyond 12 m (forty feet) of each other’s fire, need about 35 m per dragon in a two-by-two layout, before any room for keepers. (B04-079, B04-081, B04-092)
- **Explicit.** The Horntail’s spread wings match a small aeroplane’s and her tail cuts yard-long gouges; a flyer climbs high enough for her to look dog-sized. (B04-092, B04-093)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It lies round the Forest perimeter beyond the horse paddock, so far that castle and lake are out of sight, behind a clump of trees. (B04-078, B04-091)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The fence is of thick wooden planks with a gap serving as the entrance; nothing roofs it, and the ground inside is hard. (B04-079, B04-092, B04-093)

### Getting there

- **Inference.** Hurrying, Harry covers most of the way back to the castle in about ten minutes. Because: He has just under fifteen minutes left as he speeds up along the edge and under five on reaching the castle, with a stop of a minute or more on the way. (B04-078)
- **Inference.** The outward stroll from the cabin is long; walk and viewing together fit within about 45 minutes. Because: Hagrid meets Harry at midnight, and Harry turns back with about fifteen minutes left before his one o’clock deadline. (B04-253, B04-077, B04-078)
- **Explicit.** On task day lessons stop at midday so the whole school can walk down to it. (B04-091)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Chains from heavy leather straps at neck and legs are made fast to iron pegs driven deep into the ground. (B04-079)
- **Explicit.** Occupants are a blue-grey Swedish Short-Snout, a smaller Common Welsh Green, a red Chinese Fireball and a black Hungarian Horntail with bronze tail spikes, plus huge grey eggs. (B04-080)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** For the 24 November task stands were conjured at the same spot, seating hundreds around the enclosure. (B04-091, B04-092, B04-158)
- **Explicit.** A champions’ tent facing the approach blocks the view of the dragons; a separate first-aid tent stands outside the fence. (B04-091, B04-094)
- **Inference.** By February 1995 the same seating stands at the lake, so the installation did not last. Because: The narrator says the seats that circled the enclosure in November are now ranged along the far bank. (B04-158)

### Open questions

- Its bearing from the castle is unstated beyond being round the Forest edge past the horse paddock.
