## Astronomy Tower

The Astronomy Tower is the tallest tower of Hogwarts, standing at the front of the castle: a parapet at its top lies directly above the front doors, and its foot is on the grass near them. A steep spiral stair rises from a corridor, floor unstated, to a door with an iron ring, which opens on an open-air top with crenellated ramparts, a parapet and a wall. Classes and exams use it at night with telescopes on stands, and pupils may go there only for lessons. It must overtop Gryffindor Tower: if that reaches a fourteenth floor, these ramparts stand at about the fifteenth-floor level or higher. Dumbledore fell from it in June 1997.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It is the tallest tower of Hogwarts and the highest of the castle. (B01-100, B01-102, B03-108, B06-175, B06-190, B07-029)
- **Reported.** It is named with Ravenclaw and Gryffindor as the three highest towers. (B07-068)
- **Estimate.** Its ramparts must clear Gryffindor Tower: at about floor 15 level or more if that tower's top floor is 14, which is about 52 m above the ground floor at 3.5 m a storey. Because: It is the highest tower, and Gryffindor's top room has its ceiling at about floor 15 level: 15 x 3.5 = 52 m. The storey height and the top floor of 14 are estimates. (B06-175, B07-068)
- **Explicit.** It holds an exam sitting with telescopes and examiners walking among them, and took four broom riders with a slung crate. (B01-101, B05-262, B06-175)
- **Estimate.** Allow a top at least about 14 m across. Because: If the whole fifth year sat there, and a year group was over 100 at a 1976 exam, then 100 x 1.5 m2 per telescope and user = 150 m2, a circle about 14 m across. (B05-219, B05-262)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** A parapet at the top stands directly above the castle's front doors. (B05-263)
- **Explicit.** Its foot is on the grass at the castle front, near the oak doors, and a fall over the battlements lands there. (B06-181, B06-190, B07-029)
- **Explicit.** The top looks over the lawn, the grounds and Hagrid's lit cabin, far enough for walkers to be hard to see, yet a roar from the cabin carries up. (B05-263)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The top is open to the sky, with crenellated ramparts, a parapet and a wall to lean on. No roof or dome is described. (B01-101, B05-262, B06-175, B06-177)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** A steep spiral stair rises from a corridor to the top, its foot reached by the marble staircase, more stairs and corridors. Harry leapt its last ten steps; ten is not its length. (B01-100, B01-101, B05-262, B06-183)
- **Explicit.** The stair ends at a door with an iron ring. (B06-177)
- **Explicit.** Pupils may go there only for lessons. (B01-102)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Telescopes stand on stands set up by the users, who carry them in holders. Astronomy exams are held here at night. (B03-108, B05-262)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** June 1997: the Dark Mark hung over the tower, casting green light, and the Headmaster fell from the battlements. (B06-178, B06-181)
- **Reported.** June 1997: a curse sealed the stair behind the Death Eaters, and a later hex brought down half the corridor ceiling at its foot, breaking the curse. (B06-180, B06-183, B06-195)
- **Reported.** The Bloody Baron is said to go groaning and clanking up there as a favourite pastime. (B06-147)

### Conflicts

- One Book 1 passage says the tallest astronomy tower, which could imply several. Every later passage has a single Astronomy Tower. (B01-102, B05-262)

### Open questions

- The floor of the corridor at the stair's foot. Death Eaters headed for it from the seventh floor, which suggests a high floor, but the books do not say.
- The tower's total height and plan. A long stair is implied if Gryffindor reaches floor 14, though none is described.
