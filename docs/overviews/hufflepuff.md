## Approach to the Hufflepuff common room

The Hufflepuff common room is never seen. The approach is a door to the right of the marble staircase, as you face it from the Entrance Hall, then stone steps down to a broad, torch-lit basement corridor that also leads to the kitchens. Map views place the Hufflepuffs in that basement, close to the kitchens.

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The steps reach a basement corridor that also serves the kitchens: broad, brightly torch-lit and hung with cheerful food paintings. (B04-106, B05-115)
- **Explicit.** The Marauder's Map places Hufflepuffs in a basement close to the kitchens, and Cedric Diggory comes up from their common room into the Entrance Hall. (B04-184, B05-115, B06-129)
- **Inference.** This door and the Slytherin door lie on opposite sides of the bottom of the marble staircase. Because: Coming down the stairs, the kitchen door is reached by turning left and the Slytherin door is on the right. (B04-106, B05-302)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The way down is a door to the right of the marble staircase seen from the Entrance Hall, which is on the left coming down the stairs. Stone steps lie beyond it. (B04-062, B04-106)

### Open questions

- The common room itself, its entrance, and the dormitories. Nothing is described beyond the corridor.
