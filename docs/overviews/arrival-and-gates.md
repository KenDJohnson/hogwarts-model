# Arrival routes, station, gates and perimeter

[Castle overview](index.md) · [All records for this area](../areas/arrival-and-gates.md)

The approach to the school, in the order you would travel it. From London, a barrier between platforms nine and ten leads to the platform for the Hogwarts Express, which leaves at eleven and runs north all day to Hogsmeade station on the far side of the lake. There the school divides. First-years go down a pine-lined path to the shore and cross in small boats, four to a boat, to an ivy-hidden opening in the cliff, a tunnel and an underground harbour, then climb through rock to grass by the front steps. Everyone else takes one of about a hundred coaches, drawn by Thestrals, along a long uphill lane to the winged-boar gates, up the long sweeping drive, and stops at the same stone steps below the great oak front doors. Both routes end at those steps. Every stage climbs towards the castle, and it is far longer than a coach ride suggests. The Knight Bus is a rare alternative that stops outside the gates.

## Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** About a hundred coaches run in convoy, three to five pupils in each, while first-years cross four to a boat in an uncounted fleet. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-012, B01-007)
- **Explicit.** Train: at least seven carriages. Road: far longer on foot than Harry expected. Drive: long enough that the castle's foot looks distant from the gates. Only estimates give lengths. (B05-005, B06-011, B06-016)

## Where it is

- **Explicit.** Order for most pupils: station, road and lane, gates, drive, steps, doors. The village lies off the same lane, left of the gates as you leave. (B04-005, B05-078, B06-054)
- **Inference.** The two routes part at the station, one crossing the lake and the other going by road, and meet again at the steps. Because: The boat route ends on grass below the steps (B01-009), and the coaches stop beside the same steps (B05-015). (B03-006, B01-009, B05-015)
- **Explicit.** Every stage rises: the lane climbs to the gates, the drive climbs to the steps, and the steps climb to the doors. Only the first-years' path drops, to the lake. (B06-011, B05-162, B01-005, B04-005)

## Getting there

- **Reported.** Gates and walls are jinxed, and Apparition stops at this boundary. (B06-015, B06-188)

## How it changes

- **Explicit.** Dated changes: Dementors at the gates from 1993 to 1994, Secrecy Sensors at the doors from 1996, and breaches of the wall in 1998. (B03-008, B06-053, B07-072)

## Open questions

- Lengths of the road and drive, and the line of the boundary wall.

## King’s Cross and the platform barrier

The London end of the school railway, included only as the start of the journey. Platform nine and three-quarters is reached by walking straight at the solid-looking barrier between platforms nine and ten. On the far side a wrought-iron archway carries the platform's name, and a long crowded platform holds the scarlet engine. The train leaves at eleven. Nothing here gives a distance to Hogwarts.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The platform is long enough that in 2017 another family stands some fifty yards along it, and it is packed with families, trunks, cats and owls on departure day. (B07-137, B01-003, B07-106)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The way in is at a ticket box in the barrier between ordinary platforms nine and ten of the Muggle station. (B01-003, B04-003, B05-004, B06-008)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Seen from inside, a wrought-iron arch with the platform's name stands where the barrier was, and a sign overhead shows Hogwarts Express, eleven o'clock. (B01-003, B05-004)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** Travellers walk or run straight at the barrier and pass through without collision. It looks entirely solid. (B01-003, B04-003, B07-137)
- **Explicit.** At the end of term a guard or ticket inspector lets pupils out through it a few at a time, so Muggles do not see a crowd step from a wall. (B01-145, B05-311)
- **Explicit.** The train leaves at eleven on 1 September, by the school letter and in the years shown, and returns to the same platform. (B01-003, B03-001, B07-137, B02-163, B04-243)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** On 1 September 1992 the barrier turns solid for Harry and Ron; they hit it and hear no train beyond. Dobby later admits sealing it. (B02-003, B02-068)
- **Explicit.** It works the same in the 1971 memory and in 2017. (B07-106, B07-137)

## Hogwarts Express

The school train: a scarlet steam engine pulling corridor carriages between King's Cross and Hogsmeade station. It leaves at eleven on 1 September and arrives after dark, running north through fields, moors, a great city and rolling hills to mountains and forest. Inside, a corridor serves compartments with sliding glass-panelled doors, luggage racks and lanterns, with a prefect carriage towards the engine, a toilet and a food trolley. It carries nearly the whole school at once and fills up, so it must be long: at least seven carriages are implied. Near the end of the run the castle can be seen from the window.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** At least six carriages are walked through in 1995, plus the prefect carriage towards the engine, and the compartments passed are already full. (B05-005)
- **Inference.** A compartment takes six: Harry, Neville, Ginny and Luna sit together in 1995 and Ron and Hermione join them. Because: Four take seats at the start of the journey and Ron and Hermione come in after prefect duties; six is the most seen, not a stated limit. (B05-005)
- **Estimate.** To carry 300 to 800 pupils at six to a compartment and eight compartments to a carriage, the train needs about 7 to 17 carriages. Not canon. Because: 6 x 8 = 48 per carriage; 300/48 = 6.3; 800/48 = 16.7. Eight compartments per carriage is an assumption, and the pupil range is itself an estimate. (B05-005, B01-151, B04-248, B05-315, B06-217, B07-140)
- **Explicit.** The journey fills the day: departure at eleven, lamps lit when darkness falls, arrival at night. (B01-003, B05-006, B04-003)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It runs north: past fields, purple moors, villages and a great city, then wilder rolling hills and a snow-capped mountain, with mountains and forest near the end. (B02-004, B03-005, B01-004, B07-011)
- **Reported.** The only named places on the flight north are London, Norfolk and Peebles, from a newspaper report in 1992. (B02-168)
- **Explicit.** The castle shows from the railway: Malfoy says he can see it just before arrival in 1996, and Harry watches it pass behind a mountain when leaving in 1994. (B06-009, B03-164)
- **Explicit.** In 1995 Harry looks for a first distant view from the train, but rain, grime and a moonless night defeat him. (B05-006)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A scarlet steam engine, heavy with smoke and steam at the platform. (B01-003, B05-004, B07-137)
- **Explicit.** A corridor runs along the carriages, serving compartments through sliding doors with glass panels and blinds. Compartments have facing seats, corner window places and swinging lanterns. (B05-005, B06-010, B07-106, B05-311)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The prefect carriage lies towards the engine; latecomers end up in the last carriages. (B05-005)
- **Explicit.** An announcement carries through the train before arrival. Luggage stays aboard and reaches the school by another route. (B01-153)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Luggage racks over the seats take trunks and owl cages. The train has a toilet and a food trolley. (B05-005, B05-311, B06-010)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** On 1 September 1993 it stops short of the station, the lights fail, and a Dementor comes aboard in a search for Black. (B03-005)
- **Explicit.** It leaves an hour after Dumbledore's funeral in June 1997, and also runs home at the end of each year. (B06-212, B02-163, B04-243, B01-145)
- **Explicit.** The carriages appear unchanged in the 1971 memory and in 2017. (B07-106, B07-137)

### Open questions

- The number of carriages, and the route of the last miles to the station.

## Hogsmeade station

The school's railhead, a very small, dark platform on the far side of the lake from the castle. Two routes leave it. First-years go down a steep, narrow, pine-lined path with a bend in it to the lakeshore and the boats. Everyone else moves along the platform and out through the station by a narrow doorway to the road, where about a hundred coaches wait. Though called tiny, it must take a full train and hundreds of pupils at once, and the crowd moves slowly.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The platform is called tiny in 1991 and 1993. (B01-005, B03-006)
- **Inference.** Small though it is, it must take a full train of at least seven carriages and hundreds of pupils at once, so make it long and narrow, not short. Because: A mass of people shunts the trio along it in 1993 and pupils inch along it in 1994, while the train has at least seven carriages. Tiny can only describe its width and facilities. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-005)

### Where it is

- **Inference.** It lies on the far side of the lake from the castle: the first-years' path ends at the shore with the castle straight across the water. Because: The path from the platform comes out at the lake's edge and the castle stands on the other side. (B01-005, B01-006)
- **Explicit.** The road and the coach stand lie just outside the station, and the lane to the school starts there. (B05-008, B06-011)
- **Explicit.** In 1996, lying on the stopped train, Harry thinks he can hear the trees around the lake and a far-off owl, so the shore may be close. (B06-012)
- **Reported.** The castle comes into view from the train just before it stops. (B06-009)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** It has a station building or wall: pupils pass through it by a narrow doorway to reach the road. (B05-008)
- **Explicit.** Pines line the path to the lake and their smell reaches the platform. (B05-007, B01-005)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** First-years gather at the far end of the platform and go down a steep, narrow path round a bend to the lake, led by Hagrid's lamp, or a teacher's lantern in 1995. (B01-005, B05-007, B03-006, B04-004)
- **Explicit.** All other pupils go along the platform to the road and the coaches. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-008)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** Arrivals are always after dark, in cold air, with driving rain and a storm in 1993 and 1994. (B01-005, B03-006, B04-004)

### Open questions

- The size of the station building, and how far the station lies from Hogsmeade village.

## Carriages and the road to school

About a hundred coaches carry every pupil above first year from Hogsmeade station to the castle in one long convoy. The route starts as a rough road outside the station and becomes a bending, hedged lane, climbing all the way to the winged-boar gates, where the drive takes over. It is far longer than it feels by coach: Harry is surprised by the distance when he first walks it in 1996. The coaches are closed, with doors, small windows, lumpy seats and a smell of mould and straw, and carry three to five pupils each in the scenes shown. Skeletal winged Thestrals pull them, though to many pupils they appear horseless.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** A hundred or so coaches: at least a hundred in 1993, a hundred in 1994, about a hundred in 1995. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-008)
- **Explicit.** Riders seen in a coach: three in 1993, four in 1994, five in 1995, when Hermione hurries in case they all fill up. (B03-006, B04-004, B05-012)
- **Estimate.** At four to five riders the fleet carries about 400 to 500 pupils, the whole school less its first-years. Not canon. Because: 100 coaches x 4 to 5 riders = 400 to 500. Assumes the coaches seen are typical. At three riders it would be 300. (B04-004, B05-012, B05-008)
- **Estimate.** In single file a hundred coaches make a convoy about 1 km long, so the stand outside the station and the road need that much room. Not canon. Because: 100 x 10 m per coach with its animal and a gap = 1,000 m. The 10 m is an assumption, and the coach stand may be double-parked. (B04-004, B05-013, B05-008)
- **Explicit.** Having always gone by coach, Harry had not realised how far the school was from the station until he walked the lane in 1996. (B06-011)
- **Estimate.** Model the road from station to gates at no less than about 1.5 km. Not canon; the text gives no length. Because: Harry's surprise at the distance suggests at least a 15 to 20 minute walk (assumption); at 5 km/h that is 1.25 to 1.7 km. It must also hold the 1 km convoy. (B06-011, B05-008)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Route order: station, road and lane uphill, gates between boar-topped pillars, then the drive to the front steps. (B05-013, B06-011, B06-054)
- **Inference.** The village connects to the same lane: from the High Street the road runs uphill to the gates, which lie to the right as you approach them from the village. Because: Leaving the gates one turns left towards the village (B05-078), so the gates are on the right coming the other way. The text never says outright that the station lane and village road are one road. (B06-054, B06-170, B05-078)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The surface is a rough mud track in the rain of 1993, a rain-washed road in 1995, and a lane with corners, bends and a hedgerow in 1996. (B03-006, B05-008, B06-054)
- **Explicit.** From the air in June 1997 it appears as a dark, twisting lane running to the boundary wall. (B06-175)
- **Explicit.** Coaches are stagecoach-style with wheels, doors and small windows; inside they are dark and musty, smelling of mould and straw, with lumpy seats. They bump and sway in procession. (B03-007, B05-012, B05-013, B03-008)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** Harry and Luna can see the animals in 1995; Ron stands right in front of one and cannot. (B05-010)
- **Reported.** Luna says they have always pulled the coaches. Hagrid says the Forest herd began with one male and five females and mainly does this work. (B05-011, B05-141)
- **Explicit.** At the end of the year coaches come up the drive to collect pupils waiting in the Entrance Hall. (B04-239)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** Each coach is drawn by skeletal black horse-like animals between the shafts, with dragon-like heads, white pupil-less eyes and vast leathery bat wings. (B05-009)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** To Harry the coaches look horseless until September 1995: he assumes an invisible horse in 1993. (B03-007, B04-004, B05-009)
- **Explicit.** October 1996: sleet, wind and frozen slush on the lane. (B06-055)
- **Reported.** Still in use in 2017. (B07-138)

### Open questions

- How many Thestrals draw each coach.
- Where a hundred coaches are kept and how they turn at the castle.
- The true length of the road and the number of bends.

## First-year boats, cliff tunnel and harbour

First-years reach the castle by boat, a tradition still running in 2017. A fleet of little boats waits at the shore at the end of the path from the station, each taking no more than four pupils, with Hagrid alone in one. They move off together on his command, cross the lake to the cliff below the castle, pass behind a hanging screen of ivy into a broad opening, and follow a dark tunnel that seems to run beneath the castle to an underground harbour of rocks and pebbles. A passage then climbs through the rock to grass in the castle's shadow, close to the front steps. No dock or building is described at either end.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** No more than four to a boat: Harry, Ron, Hermione and Neville share one, and Hagrid has one to himself. The boats are small and form a fleet. (B01-007)
- **Estimate.** At four to a boat, 1991's 24-plus first-years need at least seven boats counting Hagrid's, and a year of 43 to 114 needs about 11 to 29. Not canon. Because: 24/4 = 6, plus Hagrid's boat. 43/4 = 10.75 and 114/4 = 28.5, from the school-roll estimate. The number of boats is never given. (B01-007, B01-152, B04-249, B07-141)
- **Inference.** The harbour must berth the whole fleet and let every first-year land, so it is a sizeable cavern, though no dimension is given. Because: The boats all arrive there and the passengers climb out together; nothing suggests they unload elsewhere. (B01-008)
- **Inference.** The rock passage must climb from lake level to the grass at the castle's foot, close to the height of the cliff. Because: It starts at the underground harbour and comes out on grass beside the castle, so it rises about as far as the cliff does. No length or step count is given. (B01-009, B01-006)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** Boats wait at the shore at the end of the path from the station, on the far side of the lake from the castle. (B01-005, B01-007)
- **Explicit.** The opening is in the cliff face below the castle; the tunnel seems to carry the boats right under it. (B01-008)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The opening is wide but hidden by hanging ivy, and riders must duck to pass. (B01-008)
- **Explicit.** The tunnel is dark and ends at a kind of underground harbour where passengers climb out on to rocks and pebbles. No quay is described. (B01-008)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The boats move by themselves, all together, on Hagrid's spoken order. (B01-007)
- **Explicit.** From the harbour a passage climbs through the rock to smooth damp grass in the castle's shadow; a flight of stone steps then leads to the front door. (B01-009)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** The crossing is described as traditional in 1993 and 1994 and is still planned in 2017. In the storm of 1994 Dennis Creevey falls in and is pushed back aboard. (B03-006, B04-004, B04-013, B07-138)
- **Explicit.** At the end of Harry's first year Hagrid takes them down to a fleet of boats to cross the lake before the train. (B01-145)

### Open questions

- Whether first-years always return by boat, or only in 1992; later years wait for coaches.
- The size and shape of the harbour and passage.

## Main gates and perimeter

The main entrance to the grounds: a pair of high wrought-iron gates between tall stone pillars, each topped by a winged boar, at the foot of the long drive. They stand on the lane from Hogsmeade, and leaving them the village road lies to the left. A boundary wall runs away from them around the grounds, far enough from the front of the castle to be out of sight. The gates can be chained and padlocked; in 1996 they resist an Unlocking Charm but open to Snape's wand. There is more than one entrance to the grounds, though only these gates are ever described. The Apparition limit runs along this boundary. Dementors flank them in 1993 to 1994, and the wall is breached by the Forest in 1998.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The gates are high, the pillars tall, and no measurement is given. (B07-065, B05-013, B06-014)
- **Explicit.** The boundary lies far from the castle front: in May 1998 people swarming over the walls are heard from the lawn, not seen. (B07-127)
- **Inference.** There is more than one entrance to the grounds: Dumbledore speaks of every entrance, and Macnair goes to one of them. Only the main gates are ever described. Because: The plural is used by several speakers in 1993 to 1994, but no second gate is seen. (B03-012, B03-137, B03-003)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** They stand at the bottom of the drive, on the lane to Hogsmeade. Leaving, pupils pass between the pillars and turn left for the village. (B05-078, B06-169, B06-170)
- **Reported.** Ron says the Marauder's Map stops at the school's boundaries, so the grounds have a defined edge. (B04-191)
- **Explicit.** From the gates one looks across dark grounds to the castle; from Hagrid's cabin they are a long run away. (B07-065, B06-016, B05-265)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** A pair of wrought-iron gates between stone columns, each column topped by a winged boar. (B03-008, B04-005, B05-078, B06-014, B07-065)
- **Explicit.** A boundary wall runs around the grounds. Walls are mentioned from Book 6 onward; height and material are not given. (B06-175, B06-015, B07-041, B07-074, B07-127)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** On the late night of 1 September 1996 the gates are chained and padlocked. Alohomora fails; Snape taps the padlock, the chains draw back and the gates creak open, then clang shut and relock. (B06-014, B06-015)
- **Reported.** Tonks says Dumbledore bewitched the gates himself and all the perimeter walls carry anti-intruder jinxes. (B06-015)
- **Explicit.** Apparition is possible just beyond this line: Snape Disapparates outside it in June 1997 as Buckbeak circles the gates. (B06-188)
- **Explicit.** Vehicles stop outside: the Knight Bus sets pupils down on the road in January 1996. (B05-160)
- **Reported.** Dumbledore tells Lupin in June 1994 that his carriage is waiting at the gates. (B03-162)
- **Explicit.** Pupils with permission walk out through them for Hogsmeade and return uphill by the same road. (B04-135, B05-078)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** September 1993 to June 1994: a tall hooded Dementor stands on each side, with others at every entrance. Fudge removes them after the attack on Harry. (B03-008, B03-012, B03-057, B03-159)
- **Explicit.** March 1998: in Harry's vision Voldemort waits at the gates before dawn, then goes up through the grounds beside Snape. (B07-032)
- **Reported.** May 1998: Dementors are reported around the walls, Grawp smashes through the boundary by the Forest, and allies swarm over the walls at dawn. (B07-041, B07-072, B07-127)

### Open questions

- Height, material and full line of the boundary wall, including whether it encloses the Forest.
- Where any other entrances are.

## Drive from the gates to the castle

The drive climbs from the main gates to the stone steps at the castle front. It is called long, sloping, sweeping and wide, and carriages gather speed on it. A lantern at the castle's foot looks distant from the gates, and the walk takes long enough for an extended conversation. It is visible from the forecourt, from the Charms classroom, and from the doors at night by the light they throw. The route passes near the lake and the Quidditch stadium.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** Called long and sloping in 1993, sweeping in 1994, and wide in 1995. No length is given. (B03-008, B04-005, B05-078)
- **Explicit.** The castle's foot looks distant from the gates, and in 1996 the walk up feels long, reaching the steps only at last. (B06-016)
- **Estimate.** Model the drive at no less than about 400 m. Not canon. Because: A lantern seen bobbing far off, a conversation that lasts all the way down, and gates that only come into view on the descent rule out a short approach. 400 m is an assumed minimum, about a five-minute walk. (B06-016, B06-169, B05-175)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It rises from the gates to the front steps, so the way out is downhill and the gates lie at its bottom. (B03-008, B05-162, B06-169, B04-178)
- **Explicit.** It runs near the lake and the stadium: pupils heading for the gates pass the Durmstrang ship in 1995 and the stadium in 1996. (B04-135, B05-175)
- **Explicit.** It can be watched from the forecourt, from where the school looks down it to the gates, and from the Charms classroom window. (B04-041, B05-240)
- **Explicit.** At night the open front doors throw light on to the drive and lawn. (B06-190)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** Its surface is not described, but it turns slippery under snow in January 1996. (B05-162)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** Coaches use it in both directions, climbing with arrivals and coming up empty to collect pupils at the end of the year. (B04-005, B04-239)

### Open questions

- Its length, bends and surface.

## Front steps and front doors

A flight of stone steps rises from the end of the drive to the great oak front doors, the castle's main entrance, which open into the Entrance Hall. Coaches stop beside the steps. The doors are called huge, great and giant, are double, and bolt from inside. They stand directly beneath the Astronomy Tower's parapet. The ground before them has held the whole school in rows with a house-sized flying carriage landing in front. From the top step the view runs down the drive and over the grounds to the lake, Hagrid's cabin and the distant Forest. A window of the Great Hall lies close by. The usual routes across the grounds start here, going down sloping lawns. The doors are blasted open in June 1997 and burst again in May 1998.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** The doors are called huge, great and giant, and are a double pair. (B01-009, B02-009, B03-008, B04-005, B04-158, B06-017)
- **Explicit.** The stairs are always just a flight of stone steps; no count or width is given. (B01-009, B04-005, B05-015)
- **Explicit.** The whole school lines up in rows before the steps in October 1994, first-years in front and Harry's fourth-years in the fourth row. (B04-041)
- **Explicit.** A carriage the size of a large house with a dozen elephant-sized horses lands just ahead of those rows, and the front three rows draw back. (B04-043)
- **Estimate.** The ground before the doors needs about 45 to 115 m of open width for those rows, plus clear space for that landing. Not canon. Because: One row per year, as the fourth-years' place suggests: 43 to 114 pupils per row (school-roll estimate) at an assumed 1 m each is about 45 to 115 m. No width is given. (B04-041, B04-043)
- **Inference.** The opening clears Hagrid, about twice normal height and three times as broad, who uses these doors, and a palomino centaur walks through it in 1996. Because: Hagrid opens the doors and walks out in B03-091; his size is given at B04-012; nothing says he stoops. No door height is stated. (B03-091, B04-012, B05-188)
- **Estimate.** A twenty-foot giant by the doors punches through an upper window and reaches inside, so that window is within about 30 ft of the ground. Because: A giant 20 ft tall reaches to about 30 ft with his arm up (assumed one and a half times height). The window's floor is not named. (B07-093)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** The front doors lie directly below the Astronomy Tower's parapet. (B05-263, B06-190)
- **Explicit.** Coaches halt beside the steps. The doors open into the Entrance Hall, with the Great Hall's doors on the right as you enter. (B04-005, B05-015, B06-017)
- **Explicit.** A brightly lit Great Hall window is close to the steps, and from outside it you can watch the Sorting. (B02-010)
- **Explicit.** From the top of the steps and lawns the view runs over the grounds to the lake, Hagrid's cabin, the Forest treetops, and the far-off Beauxbatons carriage. (B03-110, B04-041, B04-240, B05-251)
- **Explicit.** The steps can be seen from the pitch, the broomshed and the Forest edge, all of which are distant. (B03-105, B01-087, B03-143)
- **Explicit.** The grass where the harbour passage comes out is just below these steps. (B01-009)
- **Explicit.** The usual routes across the grounds start at the foot of the steps: down lawns to the cabin, the pitch, the lake, the Forest edge, and past the vegetable patch. (B01-040, B03-071, B05-122, B04-056, B06-137, B04-091)

### Shape and structure

- **Explicit.** The doors are heavy oak, with a lintel above the opening, and the steps are stone. (B05-251, B07-092, B04-005)

### Getting there

- **Explicit.** The doors and steps are the everyday way in and out for pupils and staff, and the whole school files out by them to the lake for the June 1997 funeral. (B06-205, B01-121, B04-170, B04-184, B04-188, B04-190, B05-171, B06-058, B06-191, B07-117)
- **Explicit.** A lock on the inside can be slid back by hand, so pupils can let themselves out at night. (B02-120, B02-113)
- **Explicit.** Filch locks the doors at night. In spring 1997 he forgets: Harry walks out at dusk, Slughorn complains of poor security, and the door is still unlocked on Harry's return. (B06-136, B06-145)
- **Explicit.** On Hogsmeade days Filch checks pupils against a list at the doors, and from 1996 uses a Secrecy Sensor on the way out and in. (B05-078, B06-053, B05-175)
- **Explicit.** In early 1994 Hagrid collects Harry and Ron from the Entrance Hall at six and walks them to his hut and back, a security escort. (B03-091)

### How it changes

- **Explicit.** 1994: Flitwick teaches the doors to recognise a large photograph of Sirius Black. (B03-088)

## Knight Bus

A vivid purple three-decker bus used once to reach the school, in January 1996, when it runs in magical jumps from London through Hogsmeade to stop on the road outside the gates. Chairs replace its night-time brass beds by day.

### Size and numbers

- **Explicit.** It has three decks, with stairs to the top and a large front window. (B05-161, B05-160)

### Where it is

- **Explicit.** It stops on the deserted road outside the school gates, after passing through Hogsmeade near the Hog's Head side street. (B05-160)

### What is inside

- **Explicit.** By day it holds mismatched chairs grouped at the windows, which slide about in the jumps. At night it holds brass beds, with candles in brackets. (B05-161)
