## B04-045: Lake and Durmstrang ship arrival

Rumbling and sucking sounds precede bubbles, waves spilling over muddy banks, and a whirlpool in the middle of the lake. A mast, rigging and skeletal-looking ship rise from it; dim misty lights shine at the portholes. Once fully afloat the ship glides to shore, drops anchor in the shallows, and lowers a plank onto the bank. The delegation walks uphill across the lawns to the castle.

Evidence: explicit; state: 30 October 1994, evening.

Model: A temporary magical arrival and mooring, not evidence for a permanent lake drain. No actual underwater route or external origin is visible.

## B04-078: Forest perimeter to hidden dragon enclosure

Hagrid leads the party around the Forest perimeter until the castle and lake are out of sight, then around a clump of trees to the dragon enclosure. On his return Harry skirts the Forest edge, encounters Karkaroff near the trees, and enters the castle front doors before climbing the marble stairs and continuing to Gryffindor.

Evidence: explicit; state: November 1994, before first task.

Model: Tree screening and route ordering are explicit; the exact enclosure bearing and distance from castle are not. The return takes Harry from under fifteen minutes remaining to under five at the castle, but his pace is unknown.

Evidence: explicit; state: Mid-January 1995 Hogsmeade visit.

Model: Confirms the gate road as the ordinary village connection and an ascending return. Passing the lake does not fix the road to a compass side of it.
