## B06-125: Room of Requirement concealed seventh-floor entrance

The Room’s concealed entrance lies in a stretch of wall on the seventh floor opposite the tapestry of Barnabas the Barmy teaching ballet to trolls. An approach corner and corridor allow lookouts to wait outside.

Evidence: explicit; state: March–early April 1997.

Model: Tapestry identifies the earlier tutu-troll landmark. Door location is precise relative to tapestry, not to a compass direction; no visible doorway when unavailable.

## B06-127: Room of Requirement mapping and requirement theory

Harry says he has never seen the Room on the Marauder’s Map. Hermione suggests it can be unplottable when required, and that Draco entered the DA headquarters by knowing precisely what room to request, whereas Harry does not know Draco’s room.

Evidence: reported; state: March–early April 1997.

Model: Preserve hypotheses as hypotheses; map omission is corroborated on the later visit at P0151. Do not make user speculation into a fixed geometry rule.

## B06-126: Room of Requirement access denial

Harry walks past three times concentrating on several requests to see what Draco is doing; no door appears. The exposed stone is solid under a push and later a kick, and repeated attempts over an hour and on another visit fail.

Evidence: explicit; state: March–early April 1997.

Model: Observed request-dependent denial. Dobby says entry is impossible, while Hermione proposes that knowing the exact required room is necessary; those interpretations are not a universal proven access algorithm.
