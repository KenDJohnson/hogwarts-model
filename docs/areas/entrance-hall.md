## B01-010: Entrance Hall

The enormous hall has stone walls lit by flaming torches, a flagged stone floor and a ceiling too high to distinguish. A magnificent marble staircase faces the arriving first-years and leads to upper floors.

Evidence: explicit; state: 1 September 1991, start-of-term feast.

Model: Scale is compared to the whole Dursley house; no numeric height or floor plan is established.

## B04-049: Entrance Hall Goblet and Age Line

The Goblet stands in the centre of the Entrance Hall on the stool normally used for the Sorting Hat. A thin golden circle is marked on the floor ten feet around it in every direction. Dumbledore says under-seventeens cannot cross; Fred and George’s Ageing Potion fails and the line ejects them onto the cold stone floor ten feet away, giving them white beards. Names dropped into the Goblet briefly turn its blue-white fire red and produce sparks.

Evidence: explicit; state: 31 October 1994, before selection.

Model: Preserve both the stated ten-foot extent around the Goblet and the separately stated ejection distance. Neither should be silently converted into a ten-foot diameter. The age restriction is explained and then observed.
