## B01-100: Hagrid’s hut to tallest tower

After waiting for Peeves in the Entrance Hall, Harry and Hermione fetch the crate from Hagrid and carry it up to the castle, up the marble staircase, through dark corridors and further stairs, using a shortcut, to the corridor beneath the tallest tower.

Evidence: explicit; state: Spring 1992, Norbert episode.

Model: Sequence and upward transport are stated; number of floors and shortcut entrance are not supplied.

## B01-101: Tallest tower — top access and aerial transfer

A steep spiral staircase rises from the corridor to an open-air tower top. Four arriving broom riders descend from the darkness and use a harness to suspend Norbert’s crate between them before flying away. Descending the same spiral returns to the corridor at its foot.

Evidence: explicit; state: Midnight Saturday, spring 1992.

Model: Open-air landing/collection area is explicit; no parapet, roof, tower plan, compass side or dimension is described.

## B01-102: Tallest astronomy tower — identity and access restriction

The previous midnight visit is identified as being up the tallest astronomy tower, which is out of bounds except for classes.

Evidence: explicit; state: Late spring 1992.

Model: Reconciles chapter 14’s tallest tower with the astronomy tower. Restricted use is a rule, not a physical lock.
