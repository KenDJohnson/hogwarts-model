## B04-004: Hogsmeade station to Hogwarts

At the stormy evening arrival, Hagrid waits at the far end of the platform. First-years traditionally sail across the lake to the castle; approximately a hundred apparently horseless carriages wait outside the station and carry the other pupils up a track towards Hogwarts.

Evidence: explicit; state: 1 September 1994 arrival.

Model: Retain Harry’s perception of horseless carriages. The paragraph gives the upward route and approximate vehicle count, not the track’s measured length or the lake landing geometry.

## B04-135: Castle to Hogsmeade via gates

The pupils leave the castle through cold wet grounds towards the gates, pass the moored Durmstrang ship, and continue down the slushy High Street to the pub. On return they run uphill along the road through the winged-boar gates and across the grounds to Hagrid’s cabin.

Evidence: explicit; state: Mid-January 1995 Hogsmeade visit.

Model: Confirms the gate road as the ordinary village connection and an ascending return. Passing the lake does not fix the road to a compass side of it.
