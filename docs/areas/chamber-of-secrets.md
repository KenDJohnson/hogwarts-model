A library page describes a huge long-lived basilisk with lethal eyes, venomous fangs, spider aversion and vulnerability to rooster calls. Hermione writes Pipes beneath it; Harry infers that the monster moves inside wall plumbing and that indirect views through camera, ghost, mirror or reflected water explain petrification instead of death.

Evidence: inference; state: Late May 1993, Chamber rescue.

Model: Explicitly a deduction from book evidence, symptoms and sound, later to be checked against admissions and observed entrance. No pipe diameter can be deduced from every wall.
