## B04-035: Owlery, West Tower

The Owlery is a circular stone room with unglazed window openings and cold draughts. Straw, owl droppings, and regurgitated mouse and vole skeletons cover the slippery floor. Perches rise nearly to the top of the tower and hold hundreds of owls. Harry takes Hedwig to a wall opening and watches her fly into the sunrise.

Evidence: explicit; state: Early September 1994, dawn.

Model: No fixed compass direction is given for a particular opening; the sunrise is a sightline in this scene. Different owl breeds share the perches.

## B04-065: Owlery rafters and school owls

The Owlery has long lines of perches, straw on the floor, and high rafters to which Hedwig can fly out of reach. School owls are kept here; Hermione says anyone may borrow them.

Evidence: explicit; state: Morning after Hallowe’en 1994.

Model: Observed structure and stock, with unrestricted borrowing as Hermione’s report. No count of school-owned versus privately owned owls is established.

## B04-180: Owlery views across grounds

From the Owlery sill Harry looks over the Forest canopy, rippling sails of the moored ship, smoke from Hagrid’s chimney, and down at Hagrid digging before his cabin. He sees Maxime leave the carriage, speak to Hagrid, and return. At dusk the owls wake and fly past him into the night.

Evidence: explicit; state: March–late May 1995; maze briefing and Crouch encounter in late May.

Model: One observed elevated panorama; it does not establish a compass bearing or visibility from every Owlery window.

## B04-192: Owlery dawn state and entrance

The misty grounds are visible from the Owlery at dawn. Half the perches are empty as owls return through the windows carrying hunted mice. Steps lead up to a hinged entrance door, and the departure stair is spiral.

Evidence: explicit; state: Late May–early June 1995, after Crouch disappearance.

Model: Adds entrance construction and morning occupancy to the earlier tower description.
