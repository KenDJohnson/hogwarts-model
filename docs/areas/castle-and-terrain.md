## B01-001: Hogwarts — historical security

Hagrid reports that Hogwarts was among the few safe places during Voldemort’s earlier rise; Voldemort did not attempt to take the school then, reportedly because he feared Dumbledore.

Evidence: reported; state: First rise of Voldemort, before October 1981; reported July 1991.

Model: A historical security claim, not evidence of a particular wall, ward, or physical defensive mechanism.
