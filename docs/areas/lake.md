## B04-162: Lake deep terrain and visibility

Beyond the visible shallows Harry dives towards the middle into a dark grey-lit, foggy landscape. Visibility is about ten feet; tangled black weed forests and broad muddy plains strewn with glimmering stones emerge suddenly from the gloom.
