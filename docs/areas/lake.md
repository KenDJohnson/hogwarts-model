## B01-005: Arrival station to lake

The train stops at a tiny dark platform. First-years follow Hagrid’s lamp down an apparently steep, narrow path, round a bend to the lakeside. Harry thinks thick trees flank the dark path.

Evidence: explicit; state: 1 September 1991 arrival.

Model: Descent and bend constrain the approach. Flanking trees are Harry’s visual supposition, not confirmed vegetation geometry.

## B04-045: Lake and Durmstrang ship arrival

Rumbling and sucking sounds precede bubbles, waves spilling over muddy banks, and a whirlpool in the middle of the lake. A mast, rigging and skeletal-looking ship rise from it; dim misty lights shine at the portholes. Once fully afloat the ship glides to shore, drops anchor in the shallows, and lowers a plank onto the bank. The delegation walks uphill across the lawns to the castle.

Evidence: explicit; state: 30 October 1994, evening.

Model: A temporary magical arrival and mooring, not evidence for a permanent lake drain. No actual underwater route or external origin is visible.
