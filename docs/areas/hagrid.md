Evidence: explicit; state: December 1994, end of term.

Model: A rear outdoor work area; the cabin itself provides shelter without establishing a roofed outbuilding.

## B04-134: Hagrid’s closed cabin state

During Hagrid’s withdrawal all cabin curtains are closed. Visitors hear Fang barking, scratching and whining behind the unopened door; knocking at a window also produces no response.

Evidence: explicit; state: January 1995.

Model: A temporary occupied-but-closed state. Failure to answer is not proof of an architectural lock or spell.

## B04-138: Hagrid’s cabin dresser and tea visit

Hagrid keeps a family photograph in a dresser drawer. During the visit Dumbledore conjures a revolving tea tray and cakes into mid-air, then places them on the table around which the visitors sit.

Evidence: explicit; state: January 1995.

Model: The dresser is an observed fixture; the tea setup is temporary. The apple tree in the photograph is unlocated and cannot be assigned to Hogwarts grounds.
