## B01-057: Great Hall evacuation to girls’ toilets

Leaving the feast, pupils climb stairs. Harry and Ron double back with Hufflepuffs into a deserted side corridor, turn a corner and hide behind a large stone griffin. Following the next corridor, they see a troll approach from a passage on the left and enter the girls’ toilets.

Evidence: explicit; state: Autumn 1991.

Model: Harry judges Snape to be heading for the third floor; this does not identify the toilets’ floor. The griffin is stone; it is not identified as a moving office entrance.

## B01-058: Girls’ toilets — door and fixtures

The toilet chamber has a door with a key left in its lock, wall-mounted sinks and taps, metal plumbing, and toilets. A wall opposite the entrance is where Hermione shelters.

Evidence: explicit; state: Autumn 1991.

Model: Key can lock the door externally. Do not add cubicles or assign this bathroom to a named ghost without evidence.

The troll knocks sinks off the walls, leaving a tap and metal pipe available to throw. Loud shouts and crashes echo, and its fall shakes the room.

Evidence: explicit; state: 31 October 1991 troll incident.

Model: Observed fixture damage and acoustics; no repair description or claim of permanent structural damage.

## B01-060: Girls’ toilets to Gryffindor Tower

After leaving the toilets Harry and Ron climb two floors before beginning to talk, then reach the Fat Lady and enter the crowded common room, where plates and the relocated feast are available.

Evidence: explicit; state: 31 October 1991, after the troll.

Model: Two floors is the ascent before conversation, not necessarily the complete ascent to the seventh-floor portrait.

## B04-131: Prefects’ bathroom directions

Cedric tells Harry the Prefects’ bathroom is the fourth door to the left of Boris the Bewildered’s statue on the fifth floor, with password Pine-fresh.

Evidence: reported; state: After Yule Ball, Christmas night 1994.

Model: Reported precise landmark and door-count directions. Left depends on the statue-viewpoint context; do not invent a compass orientation or statue pose until directly described.
