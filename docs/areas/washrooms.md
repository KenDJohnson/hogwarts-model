## B01-057: Great Hall evacuation to girls’ toilets

Leaving the feast, pupils climb stairs. Harry and Ron double back with Hufflepuffs into a deserted side corridor, turn a corner and hide behind a large stone griffin. Following the next corridor, they see a troll approach from a passage on the left and enter the girls’ toilets.

Places: Great Hall, Corridors in general, Suits of armour and statues, Girls’ toilets of the troll attack.

Evidence: explicit; state: Autumn 1991.

Model: Harry judges Snape to be heading for the third floor; this does not identify the toilets’ floor. The griffin is stone; it is not identified as a moving office entrance.
