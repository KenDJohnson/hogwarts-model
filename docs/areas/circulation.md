## B01-021: Great Hall to Gryffindor Tower

The route leaves the Great Hall, climbs the marble staircase, follows portrait-lined corridors, twice passes through doorways concealed behind sliding panels and hanging tapestries, and climbs additional staircases. The portraits whisper and point; suits of armour stand along the subsequent route.

Places: Great Hall, Marble staircase, Hidden stairs and shortcuts inside the castle, Gryffindor Tower.

Evidence: explicit; state: 1 September 1991, start-of-term feast.

Model: Sequence is explicit; exact turns, floor count and allocation of the two hidden doorways to panel/tapestry mechanisms remain open.
